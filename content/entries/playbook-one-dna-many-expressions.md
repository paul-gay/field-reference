## The technique
Instead of listing models, present the collection as **one DNA** expressed several ways: sizes, materials, movements or purposes. The shared codes reassure; the variations invite the client to find their own version.

## How to use it
1. Name the shared DNA first: the design codes every version has.
2. Lay out the expressions side by side.
3. Ask which fits their life, not which is "best."

## How it transfers to Leica
- **The M family:** one DNA (the M mount, the rangefinder idea, the handcrafted body) in several expressions: M11, M11-P, M11-D, Monochrom, M EV1, and the film Ms.
- **The Q family:** one body and sensor in three expressions: Q3, Q3 43, Q3 Monochrom. This is exactly how the Fit finder works.
- **Watches:** hand wound ZM 1 and ZM 2, automatic ZM 11 and ZM 12.
