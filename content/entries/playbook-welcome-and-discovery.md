## Welcome
- Every visitor should feel warmly welcomed. You don't get a second chance at a first impression.
- Welcome people the way you'd want to be welcomed yourself.
- A great welcome shows **energy**, creates a **genuine connection**, carries the **brand's values**, and is **inclusive**: everyone belongs, exactly as they are.

## Discovery
Combine three things: what you **observe**, what you **hear**, and what you **know** about the brand. The goal is a real dialogue.

Find out:
- What motivated the visit today.
- Who they are, what they value, what they need.
- What passions you might share.

Discovery also sets your **tone of voice** for the rest of the conversation.

## Know your own story
Take a minute to answer this for yourself: what values and passions do you personally share with the brand and its clients? Clients connect with advisors who clearly love what they sell.
