The Q family are full frame cameras with a fixed, non interchangeable Leica lens and fast autofocus. They pair a large 60MP sensor with a lens designed specifically for it, in a body small enough to carry every day. For many clients the Q is their first Leica and the camera that makes the brand feel accessible without compromising the image.

The Q's big idea is freedom from choice. There's no lens to pick, no system to plan. You pick up the camera and photograph.

> **The watch analogy**
>
> If the M is a hand wound manual watch, the Q is a beautifully finished automatic: the same craftsmanship and design, with the convenience handled for you.
