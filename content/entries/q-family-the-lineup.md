Model| Lens and key facts| Who it suits  
---|---|---  
Q3| Summilux 28 f/1.7 ASPH. 60MP full frame, Triple Resolution, 8K video, tilting touchscreen, 5.76 million dot OLED viewfinder, hybrid autofocus, macro to 17cm, digital crops to 35, 50, 75 and 90mm, IP52.| Anyone who loves wide, environmental photos: travel, street, family, architecture.  
Q3 43| APO-Summicron 43 f/2 ASPH. Same 60MP body. Introduced September 2024 at $6,985.| People who find 28mm too wide. 43mm feels close to how the eye sees, great for portraits and everyday life.  
Q3 Monochrom| Summilux 28 f/1.7 with a 60MP black and white only sensor, ISO up to 200,000, no red dot, first Q with Content Credentials. $7,790 at launch in November 2025.| Black and white devotees, street photographers, and anyone who loves the purity of monochrome.  
  
> **Reading the numbers**
>
> At launch, the Q3 Monochrom carried a $1,055 premium over the standard Q3. Prices have since moved with tariffs, so always quote from the current US list.
