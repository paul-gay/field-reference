Concept| Explanation  
---|---  
ISO| Amplification of the sensor signal (or the sensitivity rating of film). Doubling ISO doubles brightness.  
Base ISO| The native, cleanest setting, usually ISO 64 to 200. Best dynamic range.  
Noise| Random speckles, worse at high ISO and in shadows. Luminance noise is grain like; color noise shows as blotches.  
Grain| Film's physical texture from silver or dye clouds. Often loved, unlike digital noise.  
Dynamic range| The span from darkest to brightest tones a sensor can hold, measured in stops. Modern full frame sensors reach roughly 12 to 15 stops; the SL3-P is rated up to 14.  
Auto ISO| The camera raises ISO as needed while holding your chosen aperture and a minimum shutter speed. Ideal for street work.
