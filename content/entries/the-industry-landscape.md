Brand| Main mounts| Positioning in a sentence  
---|---|---  
Canon| RF (full frame and APS-C)| The long time market leader, broad range, strong for sports and events.  
Nikon| Z| Optical heritage, strong professional bodies and lenses.  
Sony| E| Mirrorless pioneer, huge lens catalog, major sensor maker for the industry.  
Fujifilm| X (APS-C), G (medium format)| Retro controls, film simulations, popular with enthusiasts.  
Panasonic| L (full frame), Micro Four Thirds| Video strength; an L-Mount Alliance founder with Leica.  
OM System| Micro Four Thirds| Compact, rugged, strong for nature and travel.  
Sigma| L and others| Lenses and cameras; L-Mount Alliance founder.  
Hasselblad| X (medium format)| Swedish medium format with a strong design identity.  
Ricoh and Pentax| K, fixed lens GR| Pocketable GR compacts beloved by street photographers.  
Zeiss and Voigtländer (Cosina)| Various, including M mount| Respected lens makers; Voigtländer offers many M mount lenses.  
  
> **How to talk about competitors**
>
> Be generous and specific. "Sony has an enormous lens catalog and superb autofocus. Leica is for people who want fewer, simpler, beautifully made tools, and a different kind of experience." Clients trust advisors who respect their other cameras.

Model lineups change quickly; this section describes brand positioning only. Check current models before comparing specifics.
