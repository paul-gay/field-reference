Part| What it does  
---|---  
Rangefinder window and focus patch| Through the viewfinder, the bright central patch shows a second image. Align the two images to focus.  
Frame lines| Bright lines showing the edges of the picture for the mounted lens. They adjust automatically for parallax.  
Frame selector lever| On the front; lets you preview other frame lines to choose a focal length. On the M EV1, it's reassigned to focus aids or digital zoom.  
Focus ring and tab| Turns the lens to focus. Many lenses have a small tab for your finger, making focus fast and blind.  
Aperture ring| On the lens. Controls how much light enters and how much is sharp (depth of field).  
Depth of field scale| Markings on the lens barrel showing the range that will be sharp at each aperture. Key to zone focusing.  
Shutter speed dial| On top. Digital and some film Ms have an A setting for automatic shutter speed.  
ISO dial (M11 and others)| Sets sensitivity on digital Ms.  
Film advance lever and rewind crank| On film Ms.  
Baseplate| On film Ms and older digital Ms, removed to load film or battery. The M11 dropped it.
