*Respect other brands out loud. Clients trust advisors who respect their other cameras.*

### "Sony (or Canon, Nikon) does more."
- **Often behind it:** Loyalty to their system
- **Say something like:** "They're excellent, and their autofocus and lens catalogs are huge. Leica is a different experience. Many people own both."
- **Watch out:** Never talk another brand down.

### "A Fuji X100 does the same thing."
- **Often behind it:** Price and style comparison
- **Say something like:** "It's a great camera. The Q gives you a full frame sensor and a Leica lens. Hold both if you can and see what feels right."

### "Isn't this just a Panasonic inside?"
- **Often behind it:** Skepticism
- **Say something like:** "Leica works with partners on some models, and we're open about it. The design, lenses, color and flagship bodies built in Wetzlar are Leica's own."
- **Watch out:** D-Lux and Sofort involve partners; say so plainly.

### "Who makes the sensors?"
- **Often behind it:** Technical curiosity
- **Say something like:** "Leica works with sensor partners and does its own processing and color. It's also developing sensors with Gpixel."
- **Watch out:** Don't guess at suppliers you can't confirm.

### "Voigtländer lenses are a fraction of the price."
- **Often behind it:** Budget
- **Say something like:** "Some are very good. Leica lenses bring tighter tolerances, coding, long term service and strong resale. Try both."

### "My phone takes great photos."
- **Often behind it:** Doesn't see the need
- **Say something like:** "It really does in good light. The difference shows in low light, real background blur and big prints. Let's try the darkest corner."
