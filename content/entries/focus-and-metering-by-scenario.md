| Scenario | Focus mode | AF area | Metering | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Portrait** | AFs, or AFc if they move | Eye detection, or single point on the nearest eye | Multi field or center weighted | The eye must be sharp; skin should be well exposed. |
| **Kids and pets** | AFc | Face, eye or animal detection with tracking | Multi field | They never stop moving; let the camera follow. |
| **Street (autofocus)** | AFc or Intelligent AF | Zone or wide | Multi field | Speed matters more than precision. |
| **Street (M camera)** | Manual, zone focused | n/a | A mode, Auto ISO with 1/250 minimum | Preset focus and let exposure take care of itself. |
| **Sports and birds** | AFc | Tracking with subject detection | Multi field | Fast, unpredictable subjects. SL3-P territory. |
| **Landscape** | AFs or manual | Single point, or hyperfocal | Multi field; check the histogram | Everything sharp, highlights protected. |
| **Backlit subject** | AFs | Single point on the face | Spot on the face, or + exposure compensation | Otherwise the bright background darkens the face. |
| **Concert or stage** | AFc | Face detection or single point | Spot or highlight weighted | Bright performers on dark stages fool multi field. |
| **Snow or beach** | AFs | Single point | Multi field with +1 to +2 compensation | The meter makes bright scenes gray. |
| **Macro and details** | Manual with magnification, or AFs | Single point | Multi field | Tiny depth of field; precision first. |
| **Night city** | Manual or AFs on a light | Single point | Highlight weighted or spot | Protect lights from blowing out. |
| **Video** | AFc with face detection, or manual | Face or tracking | Multi field; lock exposure | Smooth, steady focus and exposure. |
