## Before 12 October  
  
  * Memorize the lens name ladder and the six eras. Practice telling the Leica story in 60 seconds out loud.
  * Watch _A Century of Vision_ , Reiner Holzemer's film for Leica's centenary.
  * Spend an hour with the Leica Century Time Travel timeline on Leica's site.
  * Shoot with a Q and an M if you can borrow or rent them. Clients can tell who has actually used the camera.

## Week 1 to 2

  * Learn the store's systems: CRM, POS, waitlist, service intake, trade in grading.
  * Get the current US price list and memorize the top 15 items.
  * Ask your manager which measures matter most and how you'll be evaluated.
  * Start your client book on day one, including anyone you bring from L&B relationships where appropriate.

## Week 3 to 4

  * Volunteer for one operational job (demo rotation, event planning, or waitlist tracking) so leadership sees your operations side early.
  * Offer to lead one team huddle on a product topic, ideally watches.
  * Ask your manager what separated the last person promoted to deputy from everyone else.
