The SL is Leica's full frame mirrorless system with autofocus, interchangeable lenses and professional video. It launched in 2015 and uses the L-Mount, which Leica opened to partners in 2018 through the L-Mount Alliance. That means SL owners can use Leica SL lenses, lenses from alliance partners, and, with adapters, M lenses and many others.

If the M is about slowing down, the SL is about capability: fast autofocus, high resolution, stabilization, weather sealing, and serious video, delivered with Leica's minimalist design and interface.

> **The watch analogy**
>
> The SL is Leica's tool watch: rugged, precise, built for professional use, and still unmistakably designed.
