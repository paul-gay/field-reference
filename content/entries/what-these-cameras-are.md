The D-Lux 8 is a compact everyday camera with a zoom lens and Leica's design and interface. The Sofort 2 is a hybrid instant camera: it captures digitally and can print on instant film, so you choose which pictures become prints. Neither is a full frame flagship, and that's not the point. They're joyful, giftable, carry anywhere cameras that let more people own a Leica.

> **Why they matter to the brand**
>
> These are entry points. A great experience with a Sofort 2 gift or a D-Lux 8 on vacation is often how someone falls for Leica before they ever consider a Q or an M. Treat these clients exactly as warmly as an M buyer.
