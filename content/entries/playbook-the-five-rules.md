## The five rules
1. **Present the product the way you'd introduce yourself.** Warm, human, a little personal. Not a spec sheet.
2. **Discovery comes first.** Understanding the person is what builds trust, and trust is what makes everything after it work.
3. **Own your catchphrase.** It should feel natural for you to say and be instantly clear to the client. Borrowed lines sound borrowed.
4. **Don't volunteer information they haven't shown interest in.** Every extra fact competes with the ones that matter to them.
5. **The magic only happens when the right product meets the right person.** Selling is matching, not persuading.

> **The spirit of it**
>
> A visit should feel like a relaxed, memorable moment for both of you. Relaxed, confident advisors make relaxed, confident clients.

## How it transfers to Leica
These five rules are the backbone of the Fit finder and the Presenting Leica guide: lead with the line to say, keep facts in a drawer, and help the person find what suits them.
