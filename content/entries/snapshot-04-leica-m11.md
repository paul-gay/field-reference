## The Story
**Seventy years of the M.** The M3 of 1954 introduced the bayonet and bright rangefinder every M still uses.

**The digital M.** From the M8 in 2006 to the M11 in 2022, Leica kept the experience and changed what's inside.

**The M11 family.** M11, M11-P, M11-D, M11 Monochrom, and the M EV1 with an electronic viewfinder.

## The Craft
### Outer strengths
- The classic M silhouette and bright rangefinder window.
- No removable baseplate: the battery loads directly from the bottom.
- Physical shutter speed and ISO dials.

### Inner strengths
- **60MP BSI sensor** with Triple Resolution: 60, 36 or 18MP.
- **Rangefinder focusing** with any M lens back to 1954.
- Built in storage alongside the SD card.
