## D-Lux 8

> **Say it like this**
>
> "This is the Leica you can take anywhere. It has a bright Summilux zoom that goes from wide to a nice portrait length, a much bigger sensor than a phone, and the same clean Leica controls as our flagship cameras. It's great for travel when you want real photographs without carrying a system."

## Sofort 2

> **Say it like this**
>
> "It's an instant camera with a twist. Every shot is saved digitally, so you only print the ones you love. You can even print photos from your phone. It's pure fun, and a print you can hand someone is a very Leica kind of magic."

Leica has a long history with physical photographs and projection; the first Leica slide projector dates to 1926. Prints are part of the brand's DNA.
