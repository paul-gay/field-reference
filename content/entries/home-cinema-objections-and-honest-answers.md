They say| Answer  
---|---  
"Why not a big TV?"| "A TV is great in bright rooms. A projector gives a far larger image, and disappears visually when it's off."  
"Is it bright enough?"| "For evenings and darker rooms, yes. In bright daylight, any projector struggles, so I'd be honest about that for your space."  
"The sound?"| "Built in speakers are fine for casual use; for a real cinema feel, pair it with a sound system."
