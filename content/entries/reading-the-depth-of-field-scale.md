On most M lenses, a central index line points to the focus distance. On either side are matching pairs of aperture numbers: 4 and 4, 8 and 8, 16 and 16. Each pair brackets the zone that will be sharp at that aperture.

## Step by step

  * Set the aperture ring to f/8.
  * Look at the two "8" marks on the depth of field scale.
  * Turn the focus ring. Watch the distance numbers that sit opposite the two 8s.
  * Stop when the zone covers the range you want, for example about 2 meters to 7 meters.
  * Leave the focus ring alone and shoot.

> **Try it now with a lens in hand**
>
> With a 35mm lens at f/8, set the focus index to 3 meters. The left 8 should point near 2 meters and the right 8 near 7 meters. That's the classic street zone.

**Meters or feet:** most Leica lenses show both. Pick one scale and stick with it so your eye learns the positions.
