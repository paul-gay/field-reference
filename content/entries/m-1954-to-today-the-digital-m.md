Year| Camera| Why it matters  
---|---|---  
2006| M8| The first digital M. Its sensor was smaller than full frame, and it was sensitive to infrared, so some black fabrics looked purple; Leica supplied filters to fix it. A rocky but important start.  
2009| M9| The first full frame digital rangefinder, 18MP. The camera that proved digital M could work.  
2012| M Monochrom| The first digital camera from a major maker that only shoots black and white.  
2012| M (Typ 240)| 24MP CMOS sensor, adding live view and video for the first time.  
2014 and 2016| M Edition 60 and M-D| Digital Ms with no rear screen, for a film like experience.  
2017| M10| Slimmed to the same thickness as the film Ms, 24MP. The body many say finally felt like a true M.  
2018 to 2020| M10-P, M10-D, M10-R, M10 Monochrom| A quieter shutter and no red dot (P), no screen (D), and 40MP resolution (R and Monochrom).  
2022| M11| 60MP BSI sensor with Triple Resolution, no removable baseplate, much bigger battery life.  
2023| M11-P and M11 Monochrom| Content Credentials (P), and a 60MP black and white sensor.  
2024| M11-D and M Edition 70| No rear screen; and a 250 unit edition for 70 years of the M.  
2025| M EV1| The first M with an integrated electronic viewfinder instead of a rangefinder.  
  
> **The pattern to notice**
>
> Leica keeps offering the same choice in new forms: more automation or less, screen or no screen, color or monochrome. The M line is less about specs and more about letting each photographer choose how much the camera does.
