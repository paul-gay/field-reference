## Recently discontinued, and where to steer
| Product | Status | Steer them to |
|---|---|---|
| M11 black and silver | Ended April 2025 | M11-P, or M11 Glossy Black Paint. The EV1 if budget matters. |
| MP silver | Ended March 2025 | MP black paint, or the M-A in silver chrome. |
| M11-P Safari | No longer available | M11-P in Metal Gray. Safari lenses still exist. |
| Q2 Monochrom | Replaced Nov 2025 | Q3 Monochrom. |
| SL2, SL2-S | Replaced | SL3 for resolution, SL3-S for speed, SL3-P for both. |
| CL, TL2 (APS-C) | Ended, no successor | Their lenses work on SL bodies in crop mode. |
| D-Lux 7, V-Lux 5, C-Lux | Ended | D-Lux 8. |
| Original Sofort | Replaced | Sofort 2. |

> **The rule**
>
> "I haven't heard anything official, and I'd never want you to wait on a rumor. Here's what you can shoot with this weekend." Then offer to call them when Leica announces.

## Verify before quoting
All prices (US increase March 13, 2026). M11 Glossy Black Paint stock. Noctilux-M 35 current price. Summicron-M 66 availability. Which Cine 1 sizes are sold in the US. Watch prices and Urban Green status. Your store's trade in and pre owned terms. Sport optics warranty terms beyond the Trinovid HD. Where a lens was made: check the box.

## First week checklist
- Shoot one roll on an M6 and one day on a Q3.
- Mount an M lens on an SL3-P with the adapter.
- Print from your phone on a Sofort 2.
- Pair FOTOS and LUX to your own phone.
- Learn the demo spot for binoculars and Cine.
