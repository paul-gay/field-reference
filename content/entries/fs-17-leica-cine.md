## Ask, show, answer
- **Ask:** Which room, and how bright? Current screen size? Movies, sports, gaming? Fixed or moving?
- **Show:** dim the lights; play 4K HDR nature or film; on the Cine 1, switch it off and the TV disappears.
- **"Isn't this a Hisense?"** Hisense is our partner on the platform. The difference is the Summicron lens, Leica's image tuning and the design. Say it plainly.
- **"Why not a 100 inch TV?"** Try getting one through your door. This fits on a console and vanishes when it's off.

**Know this:** reviewers find the Cine 1 speakers modest; offer a soundbar. Room light matters more than lumens. Confirm which Cine 1 sizes are current in the US.
