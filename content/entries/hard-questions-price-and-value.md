*The most common hard moment. Never apologize for the price, and never argue about it.*

### "Why would anyone pay this much for a camera?"
- **Often behind it:** Curiosity, sometimes a test
- **Say something like:** "It's a fair question. Want to hold one and decide for yourself?" Then one craft fact: about 45 M cameras are made a day in Wetzlar.
- **Watch out:** Then stop talking. Silence lets them think.

### "I can get the same specs for half the price."
- **Often behind it:** Comparing on paper
- **Say something like:** "On features per dollar, you're right. Leica is about fewer, simpler, beautifully made tools that last decades. Hold both and tell me which you'd actually carry."

### "Is it an investment?"
- **Often behind it:** Justifying the spend
- **Say something like:** "I'd never promise any camera goes up in value. What I can say is that Leica tends to hold value well, and there's a deep secondhand and collector market."
- **Watch out:** Never promise appreciation.

### "Can you give me a discount?"
- **Often behind it:** Testing, or a real budget gap
- **Say something like:** "Let me check what we can do." Then follow store policy exactly: financing, trade in, test drive or bundle offers if they exist.
- **Watch out:** Confirm policy with your manager before promising anything.

### "It's cheaper online or in Japan."
- **Often behind it:** Price shopping
- **Say something like:** "It might be. What you get here is an authorized warranty, setup, service, events and someone who'll answer when you call."
- **Watch out:** Don't disparage other dealers; explain what's included here.

### "I'll just buy used on eBay."
- **Often behind it:** Budget
- **Say something like:** "That can work. Check for haze, fungus and rangefinder alignment. Leica Pre-Owned and Service Certificates take the guesswork out."
- **Watch out:** Helping them buy used well builds trust for later.

### "Why is the lens more expensive than the camera?"
- **Often behind it:** Surprise
- **Say something like:** "The lens is the part you keep for decades. Bodies change; a great lens goes with you to every future M."

### "The price went up since last month."
- **Often behind it:** Frustration
- **Say something like:** "It has. Leica has pointed to exchange rates and customs costs. I understand it's frustrating."
- **Watch out:** Acknowledge; don't speculate about future prices.

### "Why does the Monochrom cost more when it does less?"
- **Often behind it:** Logic puzzle
- **Say something like:** "It's a specialized sensor made in smaller numbers. Doing less, better, is the whole idea."
