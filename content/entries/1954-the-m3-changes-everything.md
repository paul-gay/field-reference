Leica introduced the M3 at Photokina in 1954. It solved every complaint in one body:

  * **The M bayonet.** A quarter turn and the lens is on. The same mount is on every M camera made since.
  * **A combined viewfinder and rangefinder.** Framing and focusing happen in one large, bright window. The M3's finder is famous for its high 0.91x magnification, close to life size.
  * **Bright frame lines** for 50, 90 and 135mm that appear automatically when you mount a lens, and shift to correct for parallax as you focus closer.
  * **Lever film advance** for faster shooting.

The M3 became the tool of a generation of photojournalists and street photographers. Its production run was long and large, and it remains one of the most collected cameras in the world. At the June 2025 Leitz Photographica Auction, M3 serial number 700000, the first production M, sold for €3.36 million, and early M prototypes have sold for more than €2 million.
