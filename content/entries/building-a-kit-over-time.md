Kit| Lenses| Who it suits  
---|---|---  
One lens| 35mm Summicron or Summilux| Most first time M buyers. Covers life, travel and street.  
One lens, alternative| 50mm Summicron or Summilux| Portrait and detail lovers who see more narrowly.  
Classic pair| 35 plus 75, or 28 plus 50| A wider view for scenes and a tighter one for people. Both pairs share frame line logic.  
Three lens travel kit| 28, 50, 90| Covers nearly everything while fitting in a small bag.  
Night kit| 35 Summilux plus a Noctilux| Clients who live after dark.  
Character kit| Classic Line Summilux 50 plus Summaron 28| Clients who value look and feel over perfection.  
  
> **Pre owned**
>
> Leica Pre-Owned and the Classic Store are great paths into lenses, especially for film shooters and clients building a second or third focal length.
