## Ownership and leadership

Item| What to know  
---|---  
Owners| ACM Projektentwicklung (the Kaufmann family) holds 55%; Blackstone holds 45%, a stake bought in 2011.  
Chairman| Andreas Kaufmann chairs the Supervisory Board. His wife, Karin Rehn-Kaufmann, leads Leica Galleries International as Art Director.  
CEO| Andreas Voll since 1 April 2026, succeeding Matthias Harsch (CEO since 2017). Voll was CEO of the fischer Group and earlier COO of IWC Schaffhausen.  
CFO| Michael Grimm, since May 2022.  
Recent change| Dr Ronny Fritsche announced in September 2026 that he is leaving Leica for a CEO role elsewhere.  
Sites| Headquarters and M production at Leitz Park, Wetzlar, Germany; a second production site in Vila Nova de Famalicão, Portugal.  
Retail| Over 120 Leica Stores worldwide through Leica's own sales companies.  
  
> **Why the new CEO matters to you**
>

Expect a luxury retail mindset from the top: clienteling, boutique experience, and category expansion into watches and lifestyle. Your watch background is aligned with where the company is heading, not a detour from it.

## The numbers (fiscal year April 2025 to March 2026)

  * Revenue was €573 million, down 3.8% from the prior year's record of €596 million.
  * Leica cited geopolitical uncertainty, unfavorable exchange rates and stricter customs rules. For a US store, read that as tariffs and price changes.
  * Photo remained the main revenue driver. Mobile kept growing strongly, Sport Optics held steady, and watches, projectors and eyecare all grew.
  * Leica expanded its store network into regions it hadn't served before, and online business contributed about 5% of the year on year revenue increase. A new Houston store fits this strategy directly.
  * For context, revenue was under €400 million as recently as fiscal 2016/17. The brand has grown a lot, fast.

## Scale and craft

The Wetzlar factory makes only about 45 M cameras a day. That single number is one of the best answers to "why does it cost so much" because it describes a manufacturing philosophy, not a markup.

## Things in the news that clients may raise

  * **Sale rumors.** In January 2026 Bloomberg reported the owners were exploring a sale of a controlling stake valued around €1 billion. Leica didn't comment, and as of late September 2026 the ownership is unchanged. Your answer: "Nothing has changed, and I'd rather talk about what's in the case."
  * **Own sensors.** Leica is working with the sensor maker Gpixel on next generation sensors, widely expected to appear in a future M.
  * **L-Mount Alliance growth.** DJI, Blackmagic Design, SIRUI and Freefly Systems have joined, which makes SL a more open system than many clients assume.
