## Five role plays

**1.** A couple on vacation from Seoul wander in, and one takes photos of the gallery wall with her phone. Welcome them and find out what they love.

**2.** A college student lingers at the M cases, then says "I'll never afford this." Make them feel welcome and give them a reason to come back.

**3.** A man asks, "Why is this $9,000 when my Sony was $2,000?" Answer without getting defensive.

**4.** A woman wants a gift for her father, who "used to shoot film." Find the right direction in under five minutes.

**5.** A retired photojournalist corrects your story about a famous photograph. Recover gracefully and learn something.

## Self scorecard after every real visit

Question| Yes or not yet  
---|---  
Did I acknowledge them within a few seconds?|   
Did I open with a question that wasn't about buying?|   
Did a camera end up in their hands?|   
Did I listen more than I talked?|   
Did I tell one story matched to their interest?|   
Could they repeat one true fact about Leica to a friend?|   
Did I offer a reason to return?|   
Did I say anything I'm not sure is accurate?|
