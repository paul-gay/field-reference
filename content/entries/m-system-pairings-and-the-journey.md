Stage| What usually comes next  
---|---  
First purchase| Body plus one lens (35 or 50 Summicron), strap, protector or half case, spare battery, SD card, extended warranty.  
Six to eighteen months later| A second focal length, often wider (28) or longer (75 or 90), or a faster lens like a Summilux.  
Established| A film body, a Monochrom, or a Noctilux. Workshops and events.  
Collector| Editions, Classic Line lenses, pre owned rarities.
