## The Story
**Prints are Leica DNA.** Leica built its first projector in 1926, a year after the Leica I. The physical photograph has always mattered.

**The Sofort line.** Leica's instant cameras bring that magic to parties, trips and gifts.

**Sofort 2.** A hybrid: every shot is saved digitally, and you choose which to print.

## The Craft
### Outer strengths
- Compact, playful design.
- Simple controls anyone can use.
- A camera people smile at.

### Inner strengths
- **Digital capture** saved to a memory card.
- **Print on instant film** only the images you choose.
- **Print phone photos** through the app.
