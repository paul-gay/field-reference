> **Catchphrase**
>
> "This is our professional all rounder: autofocus, speed and 8K video, in the simplest professional body Leica has made."

**Then pause.** Ask what a typical shoot looks like. Pros want to talk about their work, not specs.

## The walkthrough
| Step | Say |
|---|---|
| Description | A 44 megapixel full frame mirrorless camera for photo and video. |
| Inspiration | The SL system has been Leica's professional platform since 2015, on a mount shared with Panasonic, Sigma and others. |
| Features | Subject tracking autofocus, 40 frames a second with autofocus, up to 14 stops of dynamic range, 8K video, and Content Credentials for proof of authenticity. |
| Style | Solid, minimal, no red logo. |
| Other | Bring your own card and edit the files in your workflow. |

## Adapt the opening
- **Wedding photographer:** Tracking locks onto faces, even in a moving crowd.
- **Switching brands:** The L-Mount means Leica lenses plus partner lenses.
- **M owner:** Your M lenses fit with an adapter.

## Put in their hands
The 24 to 70mm zoom, eye tracking on their companion, then a short burst.

## Skip unless asked
Codec details and menus, unless they're a filmmaker.

## Scene: Sam, a wedding photographer switching systems
- **Sam:** I need autofocus I can trust in a dark church.
- **You:** Let's test it in our darkest corner. Follow me.
- **Sam:** It hasn't let go of your face once.
- **You:** Take a card's worth home and edit it tonight.
