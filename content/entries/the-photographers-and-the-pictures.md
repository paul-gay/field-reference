* **Henri Cartier-Bresson** worked with Leicas for decades and defined "the decisive moment," the idea that a photograph captures a fleeting instant when form and meaning align.
  * **Alberto Korda** made the 1960 portrait of Che Guevara usually credited to a Leica M2.
  * **Garry Winogrand** , **Joel Meyerowitz** and **Elliott Erwitt** are closely associated with Leica rangefinders in street photography.
  * Countless photojournalists carried Ms through wars, protests and daily life because the cameras were small, quiet and reliable.

Leica honors this lineage through the Leica Hall of Fame Award and the Leica Oskar Barnack Award, and through its campaign line "The World Deserves Witnesses."

> **Caution**
>
> Camera attributions for famous images are often debated among enthusiasts. Stick to well documented associations, and if a client corrects you, thank them.
