*Stay warm, stay honest, keep your boundaries.*

### "I know more about Leica than you."
- **Often behind it:** Expert, or testing you
- **Say something like:** "You might, and I'd love to learn. What are you shooting with?"
- **Watch out:** Humility beats bluffing every time.

### "That's wrong." (correcting a fact)
- **Often behind it:** Expertise
- **Say something like:** "Thank you, I'll check that." Then actually check it.
- **Watch out:** Never defend a fact you aren't sure of.

### A question you can't answer
- **Often behind it:** Expert detail
- **Say something like:** "I want to give you the right answer, so let me find out."
- **Watch out:** Never guess. Follow up.

### "Your service took forever."
- **Often behind it:** Real frustration
- **Say something like:** "I'm sorry. Let me look at where it stands and explain the next steps." Listen before explaining.
- **Watch out:** Escalate to your manager if needed.

### A rude or aggressive visitor
- **Often behind it:** Stress, a bad day, or worse
- **Say something like:** Stay calm and polite. "I want to help. Let's keep this respectful."
- **Watch out:** Your safety matters. Bring in your manager or follow store procedure.

### "Just looking."
- **Often behind it:** Wants space
- **Say something like:** "Perfect, that's what we're here for. Everything can come out, so just wave me over."
- **Watch out:** Then actually give them space.
