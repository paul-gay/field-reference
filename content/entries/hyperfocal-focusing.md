The **hyperfocal distance** is the focus setting that makes everything sharp from about half that distance to infinity. Set the infinity mark on the focus ring against your aperture number on the depth of field scale, and read the near limit on the other side.  
  
Lens| f/8 hyperfocal| Sharp from (approx.)| f/11 hyperfocal| Sharp from (approx.)  
---|---|---|---|---  
28mm| 3.3 m| 1.7 m to infinity| 2.4 m| 1.2 m to infinity  
35mm| 5.1 m| 2.6 m to infinity| 3.7 m| 1.9 m to infinity  
50mm| 10.5 m| 5.3 m to infinity| 7.6 m| 3.8 m to infinity  
  
Use it for landscapes, cityscapes, and street scenes with depth where you want both a near subject and the background sharp.
