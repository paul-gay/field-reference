* The M3 (1954) introduced the M bayonet, not the first Leica. The first Leica was the Leica I of 1925.
  * The M EV1 has no rangefinder. Don't call it a rangefinder camera.
  * Don't claim the M11-D has no electronics. It's digital; it simply has no rear screen.
  * The M-A has no battery; the M6 and MP need a battery only for the light meter.
  * M12 is a rumor. Never promise it.
  * Check current US prices and M EV1 availability before quoting.
