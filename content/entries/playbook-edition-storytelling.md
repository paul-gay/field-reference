## Types of special pieces
- **Reissues:** faithful recreations of historic designs, made in small numbers for true enthusiasts.
- **Capsule or limited collections:** eye catching pieces available for a limited time.
- **Collaborations:** co designed with a partner, combining two cultures.

## The rule
**Always connect design to origin.** Every icon has a reason it looks the way it does, and that reason is the story.

## Say it honestly
Mention limited availability as a fact, never as pressure.

## How it transfers to Leica
- **Classic Line lenses** (Summaron-M 28, Classic Summilux-M 50, Summicron-M 66): reissues and reinterpretations. Connect each to its historic original.
- **Leica editions and finishes:** tell the story behind each one, and never promise allocation you can't confirm.
- **Design to origin:** the red dot, the rangefinder window, the M bayonet. Each exists for a reason.
