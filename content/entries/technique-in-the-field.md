* **Pick a stage.** Find good light or an interesting background, set your zone to its distance, and wait for someone to walk into it.
  * **Move your feet, not the ring.** Keep the zone fixed and step closer or back so subjects fall inside it.
  * **Two presets.** Some photographers switch between a near preset (2 m) and a far preset (5 m) with a small twist of the focus tab, learning the positions by feel.
  * **Keep the camera ready.** Strap on wrist, camera on, lens cap off. With a rangefinder, check the cap; the finder can't see it.
  * **Shooting without the viewfinder.** Some shoot from chest height. It's a real technique, but framing gets looser, and it's never an excuse to be sneaky. See the etiquette section.
  * **Verify, then trust.** Check a few frames at the start of a walk, then stop reviewing and keep shooting.
