Book| Author| Why read it  
---|---|---  
The Decisive Moment (Images à la Sauvette)| Henri Cartier-Bresson| The philosophy behind Leica street photography.  
The Americans| Robert Frank| A landmark photobook made largely with a Leica.  
On Photography| Susan Sontag| Essays on what photographs mean.  
Camera Lucida| Roland Barthes| A personal meditation on photography.  
The Nature of Photographs| Stephen Shore| How photographs work visually.  
The Photographer's Eye| Michael Freeman| Composition, clearly explained.  
Understanding Exposure| Bryan Peterson| The most approachable exposure book.  
The Camera, The Negative, The Print| Ansel Adams| The classic technical trilogy.  
  
## Habits of an expert

  * Shoot every day, even ten frames.
  * Study one great photographer each month and try their approach.
  * Visit galleries, including the Leica Galleries, and museum photography departments.
  * Read LFI and follow the Leica Oskar Barnack Award winners.
  * Print your work; printing teaches more than screens.
  * Teach someone else. Explaining is the fastest way to find gaps in your own knowledge.
