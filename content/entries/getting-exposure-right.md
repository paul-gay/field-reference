## Digital M

  * Set the aperture you chose for the zone (f/8).
  * Set ISO to Auto, with a minimum shutter speed of 1/250 or faster so walking people are sharp.
  * Shutter dial to A (automatic), or set it manually.
  * In dimmer light, allow Auto ISO to go high; a little grain beats motion blur.

## Film M (ISO 400 film)

Light| f/8| f/11  
---|---|---  
Bright sun| 1/2000| 1/1000  
Hazy sun| 1/1000| 1/500  
Overcast| 1/500| 1/250  
Open shade| 1/250| 1/125  
  
Derived from the sunny 16 rule (f/16 at 1/ISO in bright sun). Film is forgiving of overexposure; when in doubt, give more light.

> **When the light is too low**
>
> If you can't get a fast enough shutter at f/8, open to f/5.6 and move your zone slightly farther away to keep it deep enough, or accept that tonight is a focus each shot kind of night.
