---|---  
With the camera| Handgrip (the Q3 grip offers wireless charging), spare battery, strap, protector, SD card, extended warranty. For Monochrom, a red filter.  
Later| A second Q with the other focal length, or an M with a 35 or 50.  
Ongoing| Firmware updates via the Leica FOTOS app, Leica Looks color profiles, workshops and photo walks.
