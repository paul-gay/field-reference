*Most doubts are fears. Answer the fear, then let them try it.*

### "Manual focus scares me."
- **Often behind it:** Fear of failing
- **Say something like:** "It feels strange for about a minute. Let me set it so you don't have to focus at all." Then zone focus: f/8, 3 meters.
- **Watch out:** Or show the Q3 or the M EV1.

### "No autofocus at this price?"
- **Often behind it:** Value logic
- **Say something like:** "That's deliberate: the M is small, quiet and fully in your control. If you want autofocus with the Leica look, the Q3 may be your camera."

### "Why no stabilization in the M?"
- **Often behind it:** Spec comparison
- **Say something like:** "The M stays slim and simple. With a quiet shutter and wide lenses, most people handhold it easily. The Q3 family and SL cameras are stabilized."

### "The M11-D has no screen. Why pay for less?"
- **Often behind it:** Confusion
- **Say something like:** "It's for people who want to shoot like film: stay in the moment and look later. It's a choice, not a shortcut."

### "60 megapixels is overkill."
- **Often behind it:** File size worry
- **Say something like:** "You can choose 60, 36 or 18 megapixels, using the whole sensor either way."

### "I can't change the lens on the Q."
- **Often behind it:** Flexibility worry
- **Say something like:** "That's the design: one lens optimized for the sensor, with framing up to 90mm. If you want to change lenses, let's look at the M or SL."

### "Should I wait for the M12?"
- **Often behind it:** Fear of buying too soon
- **Say something like:** "Leica hasn't announced one, and I'd never have you buy around a rumor. Your lenses carry forward to any future M."
- **Watch out:** Never confirm or predict unannounced products.

### "Is the M EV1 available?"
- **Often behind it:** Wants it now
- **Say something like:** "Let me check today's status for you."
- **Watch out:** Its US launch was delayed; check before promising.

### "Can I buy the Leitzphone here?"
- **Often behind it:** Saw it online
- **Say something like:** "Not in the US, unfortunately. The LUX app and grip bring a lot of that experience to iPhone."
