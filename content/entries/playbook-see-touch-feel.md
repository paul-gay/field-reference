## The technique
Don't just hand over the product. Direct the experience in three steps:
1. **See.** Point to the design codes and hidden details worth noticing.
2. **Touch.** The finishes, edges and controls under the fingers.
3. **Feel.** Comfort, weight, and the emotion of wearing or using it.

## Sound counts too
High end mechanisms have a deliberate feel and sound, the way a luxury car door closes. Let the client hear it and name it for them.

## How it transfers to Leica
| Sense | Leica M | Leica Q3 |
|---|---|---|
| **See** | The rangefinder patch snapping into one image; the engraving on the top plate; the frame lines | The crop frames appearing; the image at full magnification |
| **Touch** | The focus tab; the crisp aperture clicks; the knurled shutter dial | The macro ring's click; the tilting screen |
| **Feel** | Solid metal weight; the quiet, soft shutter sound | How light it feels in one hand |

Say it out loud: "Listen to that shutter. That's the sound of a camera built by hand."
