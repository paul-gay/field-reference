### Mirrorless and the EVF

Light goes straight to the sensor, and you see a live electronic preview of the exact exposure. What you see is what you get.

### The L-Mount Alliance

Founded in 2018 with Panasonic and Sigma, the alliance now includes companies such as DJI, Blackmagic Design, SIRUI and Freefly Systems. For clients, it means a big choice of lenses and cinema gear on the same mount.

### Resolution versus speed

More megapixels means more detail and cropping room. Fewer, larger pixels usually mean better low light and faster readout. That's the logic behind SL3 (60MP), SL3-P (44MP) and SL3-S (24MP).

### Multishot

On a tripod, the SL3-P shifts its sensor between several exposures and combines them into a 176MP image, ideal for art reproduction and architecture.

### Content Credentials

The SL3-P can sign images so viewers can verify origin and edits, important for journalism and commercial clients.
