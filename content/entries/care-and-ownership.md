* Rangefinder calibration can drift after a knock. If focus seems off at wide apertures, have it checked by Leica service.
  * Keep lens and body caps on when stored; dust on a digital sensor shows at small apertures.
  * Film Ms benefit from periodic service. Leica offers M-System service and official Service Certificates.
  * Store in a dry place; humidity is the enemy of lenses.
  * Register products in a Leica Account and consider extended warranty for digital bodies.
