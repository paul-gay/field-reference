- **Q, everything in one:** autofocus, a fixed Leica lens, stabilization and sealing, which means you just pick it up and go.
- **SL, a complete system:** fast AF, video, IBIS and the L-Mount, which means one body grows with you, and your M lenses come along.
- **Compact and instant, the easy yes:** small, simple and fun, which means a Leica that lives in a jacket pocket or at the party.

| Model | Pitch | Feature → benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Q3 | "One Leica that does everything." | 28mm f/1.7 → wide scenes, low light, background blur. Crops to 90mm → five framings. Fast AF with subject recognition → kids, pets, travel stay sharp. IP52 → rain and beach days. Macro ring → food, flowers, details. |
| Q3 43 | "The Q that sees the way you do." | 43mm APO → natural perspective, flattering faces. Crops to 120mm → portrait reach. APO → no color fringes. Same sealed, stabilized body. |
| Q3 Monochrom | "Black and white, with autofocus." | B&W sensor → deeper tones. ISO to 200,000. AF and fixed lens → Monochrom quality at a far lower price than an M. Content Credentials. |
| D-Lux 8 | "A Leica for every trip." | 24 to 75mm zoom. OLED viewfinder → compose in bright sun. DNG raw. Flash included. |
| SL3-S | "Built for speed and video." | 24MP → cleaner low light, lighter files. 30fps with AF. Internal ProRes, 6K open gate, timecode. Content Credentials. |
| SL3-P | "Resolution and speed, one body." | 44MP with Leica's best AF. Recognizes people, animals, cars. Up to 40fps. Camera to cloud. |
| SL3 | "Maximum detail." | 60MP with Triple Resolution. IBIS, M lenses included. CFexpress plus SD backup. IP54. |

> **Match the benefit to the person**
>
> A parent hears "fast AF keeps kids sharp." A traveler hears "sealed against rain." A pro hears "backup to two cards." Same camera, different reason.
