---

Part| Job| Leica example  
---|---|---  
Lens| Gathers light and focuses it into an image.| Summicron-M 35 f/2 ASPH.  
Aperture| An adjustable opening inside the lens that controls how much light passes.| The aperture ring on an M lens.  
Shutter| Opens for a set time to let light reach the sensor or film.| Focal plane shutter in the M11; leaf shutter in the Q3's lens.  
Sensor or film| Records the light.| 60MP BSI sensor in the M11; 35mm film in an M6.  
Viewfinder or screen| Lets you frame and focus.| Rangefinder in the M11; EVF in the M EV1 and Q3.  
  
Everything else, from autofocus to menus to color profiles, helps these five parts work better. The camera obscura, a dark room with a small hole projecting an image onto the opposite wall, demonstrated the principle centuries before photography existed.
