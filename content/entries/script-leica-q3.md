> **Catchphrase**
>
> "I think you'll love this one. It's a full frame Leica with one incredible lens built in, made so you never leave your camera at home."

**Then pause.** Watch their hands and eyes. If they reach for it, hand it over. If they hesitate, ask what they usually photograph.

## The walkthrough
| Step | Say |
|---|---|
| Description | It's a 60 megapixel full frame camera with a Summilux 28 lens and fast autofocus. |
| Inspiration | It's the original Leica idea from 1913: a serious camera small enough to always have with you. |
| Features | The lens is designed for this sensor, so everything is sharp. Press here and it frames like a 35, 50, 75 or 90. Twist this ring and it focuses as close as 17 centimeters. |
| Style | Understated. Nothing on it that doesn't need to be there. |
| Other | We run photo walks here. It's the best way to get to know it. |

## Adapt the opening
- **New to Leica:** You've never held a Leica? This is the perfect place to start.
- **Came for the Q3:** You've done your research. Let me show you the two things reviews can't: the macro ring and how light it feels.
- **Owns an M:** A lot of M owners keep one of these as their everyday camera.

## Put in their hands
Turn it on in full auto first. Let them take one photo, then show it at full magnification.

## Skip unless asked
Sensor specs beyond 60MP, video details, menu settings.

## Scene: Priya, visiting with her partner, looking at the cases
- **You:** Have you been to a Leica Store before?
- **Priya:** No, I just love the window.
- **You:** Then let me show you the camera most people fall for first. I think you'll love this one...
- **Priya:** (takes a photo) Wait, that's so sharp.
- **You:** Now twist this ring and photograph your ring. That's macro.
