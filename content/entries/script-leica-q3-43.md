> **Catchphrase**
>
> "Let me show you something special: the Q with an APO lens that sees the world the way your own eye does."

**Then pause.** Listen for who they photograph. If people come up, you're in the right place.

## The walkthrough
| Step | Say |
|---|---|
| Description | The same full frame Q body with a 43mm APO-Summicron lens. |
| Inspiration | 43mm is close to natural human vision, so faces look like themselves, never stretched. |
| Features | APO correction keeps edges clean. It frames all the way to 150mm, so you can do portraits from across a room. Macro to 27 centimeters. |
| Style | Natural, quiet, flattering. |
| Other | Leica Looks let you choose a color style in the app. |

## Adapt the opening
- **New to Leica:** If you mostly photograph people, this is the Leica I'd start with.
- **Came for the Q3:** Before you decide, let me take your portrait with both. It takes a minute.
- **Parent:** This is the camera for photographing your kids growing up.

## Put in their hands
Photograph their companion from the same spot with the Q3 and the Q3 43. Show both side by side.

## Skip unless asked
Crop resolution numbers, lens element counts.

## Scene: Marcus, a father of two, deciding between the Q3 and Q3 43
- **Marcus:** Everyone says get the 28.
- **You:** It's wonderful for places. Can I ask what you photograph most?
- **Marcus:** Honestly? My kids.
- **You:** Let me show you something special... (portrait with both)
- **Marcus:** The 43 looks like them.
