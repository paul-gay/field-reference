---
title: "Handling the hard moments"
category: "Selling"
tags: ["Presenting Leica"]
source: "Presenting Leica"
summary: "Stay warm, stay honest, and never argue. The goal is that they leave respecting the brand, even if they still disagree."
---

Moment| A graceful response  
---|---  
"Why would anyone pay this much?"| Smile, don't defend. "It's a fair question. Want to hold one and see what you think?" Then the craft story. Some people won't be convinced, and that's fine.  
"Isn't it just the red dot?"| "The dot's famous, but some of our most serious photographers pick the versions without it." Show an M11-P.  
"My phone takes great photos."| "It really does. The difference shows in low light, in background blur, and in how it feels to shoot." Offer a quick Q3 comparison in a dim corner.  
"Isn't Leica just Panasonic?"| "Leica partners with other companies on some models, and we're open about that. The lenses, design, color and flagship bodies built in Wetzlar are Leica's own."  
"I read Leica is being sold."| "There were reports earlier this year, but ownership hasn't changed." Then return to the product.  
A visitor knows more than you| Enjoy it. "I'd love to hear how you use that lens." Expertise shared with humility builds more trust than bluffing.  
Someone filming content in the store| Follow store policy on filming. Welcome them warmly, protect other clients' privacy, and use it as a chance to tell a story on camera if permitted.  
Children touching the cases| Engage the child kindly and include the parent: "Want to see how a camera works?" A Sofort 2 is perfect here.  
You don't know the answer| "I want to give you the right answer, so let me check." Then check. Never guess.
