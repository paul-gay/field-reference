Method| How it works| Leica example  
---|---|---  
Rangefinder| Two images align in a patch when in focus.| M11, M6.  
Split image and microprism| Classic SLR focusing screens that break or shimmer when out of focus.| Historic Leica R cameras.  
Focus peaking| Colored outlines highlight in focus edges on a screen.| M EV1, M11 live view.  
Magnification| Zooms in on the preview for precise manual focus.| M EV1, SL3.  
Contrast detect AF| Adjusts focus until contrast peaks. Accurate, sometimes slower.| Earlier compacts.  
Phase detect AF| Measures the direction and distance to focus. Fast, good for tracking.| Hybrid systems in the Q3 and SL3-P.  
Subject detection| Recognizes eyes, faces, animals and vehicles to guide AF.| Modern SL and Q cameras.  
Setting| Meaning  
---|---  
Single AF (AF-S)| Focuses once and locks. For still subjects.  
Continuous AF (AF-C)| Keeps adjusting. For moving subjects.  
AF area modes| Single point, zone, wide or tracking.  
Back button focus| Moving AF to a rear button so the shutter only takes the picture.  
Zone and hyperfocal focusing| Presetting focus so a band of distance is sharp (see the Zone Focusing guide).
