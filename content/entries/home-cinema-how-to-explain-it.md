## Key ideas in plain words

Term| Meaning  
---|---  
Laser light source| Long life and consistent brightness compared with lamps.  
Ultra short throw| Sits a few inches from the wall instead of across the room.  
Lumens| Brightness. Projectors look best in darker rooms; the Compact is ideal for evening use.  
Pixel shift 4K| The projector shifts pixels rapidly to create a 4K image from a smaller chip.  
HDR| Brighter highlights and deeper shadows, for supported content.
