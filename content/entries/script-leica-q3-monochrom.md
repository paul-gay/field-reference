> **Catchphrase**
>
> "This one is for people who already see in black and white. It's a Q that can't see color at all, and that's exactly why it's so beautiful."

**Then pause.** People either light up or look puzzled. Both are good: light up means show them; puzzled means tell the why.

## The walkthrough
| Step | Say |
|---|---|
| Description | A full frame Q with a sensor made only for black and white. |
| Inspiration | Leica made the first black and white only digital camera in 2012. This brings that idea to the Q. |
| Features | With no color filter, every pixel records pure light, so tones are richer and high ISO looks like film grain. It records black and white video too. |
| Style | All black, no red dot. Nothing to notice. |
| Other | A red filter turns skies dramatic. |

## Adapt the opening
- **Street photographer:** It's the most discreet Q we make.
- **Film lover:** If you miss Tri-X, you'll feel at home.
- **Curious:** Removing color sounds like a limit. Let me show you why it isn't.

## Put in their hands
Photograph them near a window at high ISO and show the tones on screen.

## Skip unless asked
ISO numbers unless they shoot at night; Content Credentials unless they're a journalist.

## Scene: Lena, who develops film at home
- **Lena:** Why would I want a camera that can only do black and white?
- **You:** Because it does black and white better than any color camera can. Can I show you?
- **Lena:** (looks at the screen) That looks like my darkroom prints.
- **You:** That's exactly who it's for.
