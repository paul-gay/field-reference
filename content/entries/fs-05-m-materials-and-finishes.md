## Explaining materials
- **Aluminum, lighter:** about 110g lighter than brass, roughly 20%. "You'll forget it's on your shoulder."
- **Brass, heft and durability:** dense and solid, the classic Leica feel. Can be refinished by Customer Care. "This is what a Leica has felt like since 1954."
- **Black paint vs chrome:** paint over brass wears to gold where your hands touch it; chrome stays even. Ask: "Do you want it to look new in ten years, or look like yours?"
- **Sapphire vs Gorilla Glass:** sapphire is one of the most scratch resistant clear materials there is; Gorilla Glass is tough but scratches more easily.

## Show them
- One body in each hand: black and silver M11-P. Let them guess which is heavier. "Same camera inside. It's about how you want it to feel and age."
- A well used MP or vintage M with brassing, if the store has one.
- Run a fingernail over the M11-P screen. Sapphire.

## Base and battery
Film Ms: the whole baseplate comes off to load film, as since the M3. M11 family and EV1: no baseplate; the battery clicks out of the bottom.

## If they say
- **"Why pay $250 more for silver?"** It's brass, plated in chrome. You're paying for the material and the feel, not just the color.
- **"Won't black paint look worn?"** It will, and that's the point for many owners. If you want it to stay new, choose chrome or a coated aluminum body.
