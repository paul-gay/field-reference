## Hand wound: ZM 1 and ZM 2  
  
Feature| Detail  
---|---  
Case| 41mm stainless steel (316L), 14.5mm high, 48mm lug to lug. Monochrom versions in black PVD coated steel. Urban Green variant added in 2025/26.  
Movement| Hand wound, 4 Hz, 26 jewels, 60 hour power reserve. Developed with Lehmann Präzision in Germany.  
Signature feature| Patented push crown: press it to enter time setting mode, which resets the seconds. A mode indicator shows when you're in setting mode.  
Power reserve| Shown by two blades that gradually close, like a camera's aperture.  
Other| Date with quick set pusher, domed sapphire crystal, sapphire case back, 50m water resistance.  
ZM 2 adds| Second time zone (GMT) with a day and night indicator.  
  
## Automatic: ZM 11 and ZM 12

Model| Detail  
---|---  
ZM 11| 41mm automatic sports watch, movement by Chronode in Switzerland (calibre LA-3001), quick change strap system, steel or titanium versions.  
ZM 12| A more compact 39mm by 13mm case, calibre LA-3002. Steel in blue orange, green black and silver grey; titanium in brown black with gilt accents. Screw down crown, sapphire case back, red dot strap fasteners for easy strap changes.  
  
> **Naming logic**
>
> Leica uses plain language names instead of reference numbers: Collection, then case material, then dial name, then strap. Teach clients to read the name rather than memorize codes.
