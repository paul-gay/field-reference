## The Story
**A revival.** The M1 of 1959 was an M without a rangefinder. The M EV1 brings back the idea for today.

**October 2025.** The first M with an integrated electronic viewfinder instead of a rangefinder.

**In the US.** Its US launch was delayed by FCC approval, so confirm availability.

## The Craft
### Outer strengths
- The M body and bayonet, with an EVF in place of the rangefinder window.
- The front lever reassigned to focus aids or digital zoom.
- Works with every M lens.

### Inner strengths
- **5.76 million dot EVF**: you see exactly what the sensor sees.
- **Focus peaking and magnification** for precise manual focus.
- **60MP sensor** from the M11 generation.

## In Hand
### See
- Peaking lighting up the sharp edges.
- Exact framing with any focal length.

### Touch
- The same focus tabs and aperture rings.
- The reassigned front lever.

### Feel
- Confidence at f/1.2 and at close range.
- All the M craft, none of the rangefinder learning curve.

- You wear glasses or find the patch hard to see.
- You love very fast or close focus lenses.
- You shoot longer focal lengths.
