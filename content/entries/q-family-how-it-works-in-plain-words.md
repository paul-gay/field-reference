### Fixed lens

Because the lens is designed for this exact sensor and never comes off, it can be optimized perfectly and dust can't get onto the sensor during lens changes.

### Crop modes

Press a button and the camera frames as if you had a longer lens. The Q3 offers 35, 50, 75 and 90mm framing. The 60MP sensor means there's plenty of detail left even after cropping, and the full image is kept in the raw file.

### Macro

Turn the ring on the lens and the Q3 focuses as close as 17cm. Food, flowers, textures, the watch on your wrist.

### Triple Resolution

Shoot at 60, 36 or 18MP depending on how much detail you need. Smaller files for everyday, full resolution for prints.
