Type| Strengths| Trade offs| Leica  
---|---|---|---  
Rangefinder| Small, quiet, see outside the frame, compact lenses.| Manual focus; long lenses and close work are harder.| M11 family, M6, MP, M-A.  
SLR and DSLR| Optical view through the lens via a mirror.| Bulky; the industry has largely moved on.| Historic R system.  
Mirrorless| EVF shows the exact exposure; advanced AF; compact.| EVF lag and battery use (much improved).| SL3 family; M EV1 (manual focus).  
Fixed lens premium compact| One optimized lens, small body.| No lens changes.| Q3 family.  
Compact| Pocketable, zoom, easy.| Smaller sensor.| D-Lux 8.  
Medium format| Big sensor, tonal depth, resolution.| Size, cost, slower operation.| Formerly the S system.  
Large format| Movements, huge negatives.| Slow, heavy, film only for most users.| None current.  
Instant| A physical print right away.| Film cost, modest quality.| Sofort 2 (hybrid).  
Smartphone| Always with you; computational tricks.| Small sensors; simulated blur; less control.| Leitzphone (not in the US), LUX app.  
  
> **Computational photography**
>
> Phones combine many frames, simulate background blur and apply heavy processing. The results can be impressive, especially in good light. Dedicated cameras capture more real information in one frame: true optical blur, better low light, larger prints and files that edit more gracefully. Say this without dismissing the client's phone.
