title: "M System: Discovery questions"
category: "Selling"
tags: ["M System"]
source: "M System guide"
summary: "Ask before you recommend."
---

* "What do you photograph most, and where?"
  * "Have you ever used a manual focus camera or shot film?"
  * "How do you feel about seeing your photo right away versus waiting?" (M11 versus M11-D, or film)
  * "Do you wear glasses, or find small viewfinder details hard to see?" (consider M EV1)
  * "Color, black and white, or both?" (Monochrom)
  * "Which focal length do you naturally see the world in? Wider, or tighter?"
