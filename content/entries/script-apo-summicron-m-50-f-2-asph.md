> **Catchphrase**
>
> "This is Leica's statement of optical precision, in the focal length where Leica began: the APO-Summicron 50."

**Then pause.** Ask whether they like a precise look or a soft, glowing one. It decides between this and the Classic Summilux 50.

## The walkthrough
| Step | Say |
|---|---|
| Description | A compact 50mm f/2 with apochromatic correction. |
| Inspiration | 50mm was the first Leica focal length; this version was designed by Peter Karbe. |
| Features | Colors focus at the same point, so edges stay clean, and detail holds even on a 60 megapixel sensor. |
| Style | Precise and pure. |
| Other | A classic travel pair with a 28 or 35. |

## Adapt the opening
- **Detail lover:** Let's look at a portrait at 100 percent.
- **M11 owner:** This is the lens that shows what your sensor can do.
- **Undecided on 50s:** Let me show you its opposite too.

## Put in their hands
A portrait at f/2, viewed at full magnification.

## Skip unless asked
Optical formulas; the photo proves it.
