Term| Meaning  
---|---  
Resolution| HD (1920 x 1080), 4K (about 3840 x 2160), 6K, 8K. More allows cropping and reframing.  
Frame rate| 24 or 25 fps for a cinematic look, 30 for TV, 60 and above for slow motion.  
180 degree shutter rule| Shutter speed about double the frame rate (1/50 at 24 or 25 fps) gives natural motion blur. Use ND filters to control light.  
Log profiles| Flat recordings (like L-Log) that preserve dynamic range for color grading.  
LUT| A lookup table that converts log footage to a finished look; SL3-P includes Leica Pure and Leica Cine LUTs.  
Bit depth and chroma| 10 bit and 4:2:2 hold more color information for grading than 8 bit 4:2:0.  
Codecs| Compression formats such as H.264, H.265 and ProRes, and raw video formats.  
Open gate| Recording the full sensor area, handy for multiple aspect ratios.  
Audio| Often the weakest link; an external microphone improves results dramatically.
