## Explaining it
- **Magnesium:** one of the lightest structural metals. That's how a full frame Q3 stays around 740g while feeling solid.
- **IP52 vs IP54:** both keep out dust and splashing water. IP54 (SL) handles splashes from any direction; IP52 (Q) handles light dripping rain. Neither is waterproof. Say "weather sealed," never "waterproof."
- **Aramid fabric (SL3 Reporter):** the same fiber family used in protective gear. Say "aramid," not a brand name.

## Show them
- A Q3 and a D-Lux 8 together. Both metal, very different size.
- A thumb over the Reporter's aramid cover.
- Wipe the SL3 screen: the coating resists fingerprints.

## If they say
- **"Can I shoot it in the rain?"** Q and SL: yes, light rain and splashes. Dry it after. D-Lux and Sofort: keep them covered.
- **"Is the Sofort plastic?"** Yes, polycarbonate and acrylic, which keeps it light and fun.
