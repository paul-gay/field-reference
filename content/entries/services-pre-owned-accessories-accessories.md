Type| Examples| Selling note  
---|---|---  
Protection| Protectors and half cases, full cases, screen protection| Suggest at checkout with every camera.  
Carry| Straps and wrist straps in leather and rope, bags| Personal and giftable; let clients choose colors.  
Power and storage| Spare batteries, chargers, fast SD and CFexpress cards| Practical must haves; a spare battery is almost always right.  
Handling| Handgrips (the Q3 grip adds wireless charging), thumb supports| Improves comfort, especially with larger lenses.  
Optical| Lens hoods, filters (a red filter for Monochrom), adapters (M lenses on SL)| Explain the photographic benefit, not just the part.  
  
## Brand Collection

Small leather goods, notebooks, card holders and other branded objects. They're ideal gifts, and a way for a visitor to leave with a piece of Leica at an accessible price.
