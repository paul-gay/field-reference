## Discovery questions

  * "What do you find yourself photographing most?"
  * "When you look at your favorite pictures, are they wide scenes or close subjects?"
  * "Do you shoot in low light often?"
  * "Do you like a clean, precise look or a softer, more atmospheric one?"
  * "What's already in your bag?"

## Three demos

  * **Focal length walk:** photograph the same spot with a 28, 35 and 50 from the same position. The client usually recognizes "their" view instantly.
  * **Aperture contrast:** photograph a person at f/1.4 and f/8 with the same lens. Show both full screen.
  * **Character contrast:** the same portrait with the APO-Summicron-M 50 and the Classic Line Summilux-M 50. Let the client describe the difference in their own words.

## Objections

They say| Honest answer  
---|---  
"Third party M lenses are much cheaper."| "They are, and some are very good. Leica lenses offer tighter tolerances, build that lasts decades, lens coding, and resale strength. It's worth trying both."  
"Why is an f/2 lens so expensive?"| "With APO designs, much of the cost is in correction: color, sharpness and consistency across the frame. Let's look at a file at full magnification."  
"I can't decide between 35 and 50."| Do the focal length walk, then ask which photos they'd print.
