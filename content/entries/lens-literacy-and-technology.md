## The name tells you the maximum aperture

Name| Typical maximum aperture| Character in a sentence  
---|---|---  
Noctilux| f/0.95 to f/1.2| The night lens. Extreme light gathering and a signature dreamy falloff.  
Summilux| f/1.4 to f/1.7| Fast and versatile, the low light workhorse. The Q3's 28mm is a Summilux.  
Summicron| f/2| The classic balance of speed, size and sharpness.  
Summarit| f/2.4 to f/2.5| Compact and more accessible.  
Elmarit| f/2.8| Small, sharp, and common in zooms and macros.  
Elmar| f/3.4 and slower| The oldest name, going back to Berek's original lens.  
  
**Suffixes and prefixes:** APO means apochromatic correction (colors focus at the same point, so less color fringing). ASPH means aspherical elements. Vario means zoom. M or SL after the name tells you the mount. So "APO-Summicron-SL 50 f/2 ASPH." reads as: apochromatic, f/2, for SL, aspherical.

**Recent lenses to know:** the Noctilux-M 35 (January 2026), which uses precision glass moulding, pressing glass at high temperature, a technique Leica has mastered in house; the Summicron-M 66 f/2 (September 2026), a reinterpretation of a historic spy lens that adds a 66mm focal length to the Classic Line; and the upcoming Summilux-SL 50 f/1.4 ASPH. and APO-Macro-Elmarit-SL 100 f/2.8, due by the end of 2026.

## Technology talking points

### How a rangefinder works (the demo that sells the M)

You look through a window, not through the lens. A small patch in the center shows a second, overlapping image. Turn the focus ring until the two images line up, and the subject is in focus. Frame lines show what each lens will capture, and you can see outside them, so you watch people walk into the frame. "You're not looking at a screen. You're looking at the world, and choosing the moment." Put the camera in the client's hands and let them align the patch themselves. That moment does more than any spec sheet.

### Monochrom

A Monochrom sensor has no color filter, so every pixel records pure light. The result is sharper black and white files with smoother tones and cleaner high ISO than converting a color file. It's the most "Leica" product idea there is: removing something to make the essential better.

### Triple Resolution

The 60MP M11 and Q3 generation can record at 60, 36 or 18MP using the full sensor, so clients who don't need huge files aren't punished for the high resolution.

### Content Credentials

M11-P was the first camera to sign images with Content Authenticity Initiative metadata that shows if a file was altered. SL3-P also supports it. In an era of AI images, this matters to photojournalists and increasingly to everyone.

### Leica Looks and the FOTOS app

Leica Looks are color profiles clients can load into Q3 and SL3 family cameras. FOTOS handles transfer, remote control, and firmware updates. Firmware updates are a service story: a Leica keeps improving after purchase.
