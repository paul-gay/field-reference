Camera| How to do it  
---|---  
M rangefinders (M11, M11-P, M11-D, film Ms)| Ideal. Use the depth of field scale on the lens; the rangefinder lets you check a distance whenever you like.  
M EV1| Same lenses and scales. Focus peaking and magnification in the EVF let you confirm the zone before shooting.  
Q3 family| Switch the lens to manual focus and use its focus ring and distance markings. Check on the demo unit how the display indicates distance and depth of field. Crop modes don't change the actual 28mm zone, but they enlarge any softness, so be conservative.  
SL system| Possible in manual focus, but many autofocus lenses use electronic focusing without hard stops, which makes preset positions less repeatable. M lenses on an adapter work perfectly.  
Any camera| The principle is universal. The Leica M just makes it especially natural.
