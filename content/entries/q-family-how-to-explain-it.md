> **20 seconds**
>
> "The Q is a full frame Leica with one incredible lens built in and fast autofocus. It's the camera you actually carry every day, and the photos look like they came from a much bigger camera."

> **Two minutes**
>
> "A lot of people come in thinking Leica means manual focus. The Q is the opposite: point, shoot, done, with a Summilux lens that's one of the best in its class. Because the lens is fixed, there's nothing to choose and nothing to carry. You get a big full frame sensor, so you can crop to a tighter view when you need it, a macro setting for close details, and weather sealing for travel.
>
> The main decision is the lens. The Q3 is wider, 28mm, great for places and scenes. The Q3 43 is closer to how your eye sees, which many people love for portraits. And the Q3 Monochrom is black and white only, for purists."
