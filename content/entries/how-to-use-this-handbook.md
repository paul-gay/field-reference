This handbook is organized from foundations to expertise. Part A explains how photographs are made. Part B covers the tools: cameras, lenses and film. Part C is craft: light, composition, genres, video and printing. Part D is context: history, the industry and buying used gear. It closes with a glossary of about 130 terms and a reading list.

Level| Sections| You can...  
---|---|---  
Foundation| 02 to 07| Explain exposure, aperture, shutter, ISO and sensors to anyone.  
Working knowledge| 08 to 15| Advise on lenses, focus, light, color, film and composition.  
Expert| 16 to 24| Discuss genres, video, printing, history and the industry with professionals and collectors.  
  
> **How to become an expert, not just informed**
>
> Knowledge becomes expertise through use. After each section, go make ten photographs that apply it. Clients can tell the difference between an advisor who has read about aperture and one who has chosen it deliberately a thousand times.
