> **Catchphrase**
>
> "May I take your picture? It's yours to keep."

**Then pause.** Take the photo, print it, and hand it over. Then explain.

## The walkthrough
| Step | Say |
|---|---|
| Description | A hybrid instant camera: every photo is saved digitally, and you print only the ones you love. |
| Inspiration | Leica has cared about the physical photograph since its first projector in 1926. |
| Features | Print from the camera or from your phone with the app. No wasted film on bad shots. |
| Style | Playful and easy for any age. |
| Other | Perfect for parties, weddings and gifts. |

## Adapt the opening
- **Family:** Kids love it, and you choose what gets printed.
- **Party host:** Leave it on a table and collect the prints.
- **Serious photographer:** It's pure fun. For serious work, let me show you the Q3.

## Put in their hands
The print itself. It does all the talking.

## Skip unless asked
Technical specs almost entirely.

## Scene: Two friends wandering in off the street
- **You:** May I take your picture? It's yours to keep.
- **Friend:** (watching the print appear) This is adorable.
- **You:** And it saves every shot, so you only print the ones you love.
