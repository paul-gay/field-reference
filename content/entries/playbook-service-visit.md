## The six steps
1. **Welcome** the client and read their mood, especially if something is wrong with a piece they love.
2. **Listen** carefully, somewhere quiet, away from other sales. Make them feel understood before discussing the problem.
3. **Explain the process**: what happens to the item, and the paperwork.
4. **Recap** the process and the expected timeline.
5. **Invite questions.**
6. **Show what's new**, once the service need is handled.

## Check it together
Test the basic functions in front of the client and note anything unusual, visible marks or missing parts. It prevents surprises and builds trust.

## Explain quotes personally
Walk the client through an estimate by phone or in person rather than just sending it. People accept what they understand.

## How it transfers to Leica
Service intake for a camera or lens follows the same pattern: check functions together, note the condition clearly, set expectations for timing, and treat the visit as a chance to reconnect, not a transaction.
