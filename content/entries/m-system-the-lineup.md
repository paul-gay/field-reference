Model| What's distinctive| Say it like this  
---|---|---  
M11| 60MP full frame sensor with Triple Resolution (60, 36 or 18MP), optical rangefinder, rear screen.| "The modern classic. Everything the M does, with a sensor that captures extraordinary detail."  
M11-P| No red dot, discreet engraving, Content Credentials that can verify an image hasn't been altered.| "Built for photographers who don't want to be noticed, and who want proof their images are authentic."  
M11-D| No rear screen at all. You can't review images on the camera.| "The closest thing to shooting film on a digital camera. You stay in the moment and see the results later."  
M11 Monochrom| Black and white only sensor with no color filter.| "It only sees light, so black and white files are sharper and cleaner than any conversion."  
M EV1| First M with an integrated 5.76 million dot electronic viewfinder instead of a rangefinder, with focus peaking and magnification. Same 60MP sensor. Recommended US price $8,995; its US launch was delayed by FCC approval, so confirm status.| "All the M lenses and the M body, but you see exactly what the sensor sees. Great for fast lenses, long lenses, close focus, or anyone who finds the rangefinder hard."  
M6| Film rangefinder reissued in 2022, with a light meter.| "The camera many photographers dream about, built new again."  
MP| Film rangefinder with a light meter, a long running professional model.| "The working photographer's film M."  
M-A| Fully mechanical film camera with no battery and no meter.| "It will work in a hundred years with no electronics at all."  
  
> **A note on editions**
>
> Leica regularly releases limited editions, finishes such as Metal Gray, and anniversary models. Know what's currently in the case and on allocation at your store, and never promise an edition you can't confirm.
