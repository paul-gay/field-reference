## Focus modes: when the camera focuses
| Mode | What it does | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **AFs (single)** | Focuses once when you half press, then locks. | Still subjects: portraits, landscapes, products, architecture. Focus and recompose. |
| **AFc (continuous)** | Keeps adjusting focus while you half press. | Moving subjects: kids, pets, sports, walking people, video. |
| **Intelligent AF** (on some Leicas) | The camera switches between single and continuous depending on whether the subject moves. | Everyday shooting when you don't want to think about it. |
| **MF (manual)** | You turn the focus ring. | Low light, macro, night scenes, video focus pulls, or when AF keeps picking the wrong thing. |

## AF areas: where the camera looks
| Area | What it does | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Single point (spot)** | Focuses exactly where you place a small point. | Precise control: an eye in a portrait, one flower in a bed. |
| **Field or zone** | Uses a larger area you position. | Subjects that move a little within part of the frame. |
| **Multi field / wide** | The camera chooses across the whole frame. | Quick snapshots; risky when there's a closer object than your subject. |
| **Tracking** | Locks onto a subject and follows it across the frame. | Moving subjects you want to frame freely. |
| **Face, eye, body, animal, vehicle detection** | Recognizes the subject and prioritizes it. | People and pets; with AFc, it keeps the eye sharp as they move. |
| **Touch AF** | Tap the screen to choose the focus point. | Fast point selection on Q and SL. |

## Manual focus aids
- **Rangefinder:** line up the double image in the patch (M11 family, film Ms).
- **Focus peaking:** sharp edges are outlined in color in the EVF or screen.
- **Magnification:** zooms the preview to check fine focus, ideal at f/1.4 and wider.
- **Zone and hyperfocal focusing:** preset a sharp band of distance and stop focusing at all (see [[zone-focusing]]).

## Useful habits
- **Back button focus:** move autofocus to a rear button so the shutter only takes the picture. Focus once, then shoot freely, or hold the button to track.
- **Focus and recompose** works well at f/4 and smaller. At f/1.4 close up, moving the camera after focusing can shift focus off the eye; place the point on the eye instead.
- **AF lock (AFL):** hold focus while you reframe.
