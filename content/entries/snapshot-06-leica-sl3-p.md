## The Story
**The SL system.** Leica's professional mirrorless system since 2015, on the L-Mount shared with partners since 2018.

**June 2026.** The SL3-P joins the SL3 (60MP) and SL3-S (24MP) as the all rounder of the family.

**For professionals.** Built for photographers and filmmakers who need a camera to perform every time.

## The Craft
### Outer strengths
- Solid, minimalist body with few, customizable buttons.
- No red logo, for discretion.
- Weather sealed.

### Inner strengths
- **44MP BSI sensor**, up to 14 stops of dynamic range.
- **819 point hybrid autofocus**, 40fps with AF, 8K video.
- **Multishot** to 176MP on a tripod; **Content Credentials**.

## In Hand
