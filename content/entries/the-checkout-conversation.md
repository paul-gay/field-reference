* Protect: "Let's add a protector so it stays beautiful."  
  * Power: "A spare battery means you'll never miss a shot."
  * Store: "This card is fast enough for the camera's full resolution."
  * Cover: "Would you like extended warranty for the years ahead?"
  * Register: complete Leica Account registration together.
  * Invite: "Our next photo walk is on Saturday; would you like to join?"

> **Tone**
>
> Offer each item once, with a reason. Never push. Clients who feel looked after spend more over time than clients who feel sold to.
