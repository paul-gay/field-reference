They say| Answer  
---|---  
"Sony or Canon have more lenses."| "They have big catalogs. The L-Mount Alliance means Leica, Panasonic, Sigma and others all make lenses for this mount, so the choice is wider than people expect."  
"Isn't this based on a Panasonic?"| "Leica shares some components with partners, which is normal in the industry. The design, color science, interface, lens system and service experience are Leica's."  
"It's heavy."| "It's built as a solid metal professional tool with weather sealing. For lighter carry, many owners pair it with compact primes."  
"Why pay Leica prices for specs I can get elsewhere?"| Let them compare the experience: the interface, the build, the files. Some will prefer other brands, and that's okay.
