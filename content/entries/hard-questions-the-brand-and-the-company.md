*Answer calmly, briefly, and honestly. Then return to the product.*

### "Isn't Leica just a status symbol?"
- **Often behind it:** Skepticism or a joke
- **Say something like:** "Some people buy it for the name. The people who keep them buy them to use every day. Hold one and you'll see why."
- **Watch out:** Smile. Never get defensive.

### "Is it really made in Germany?"
- **Often behind it:** Authenticity
- **Say something like:** "Flagship M cameras and many lenses are made in Wetzlar. Leica also has a factory in Portugal, and some compacts are developed with partners."

### "I read Leica is being sold."
- **Often behind it:** News
- **Say something like:** "There were reports earlier this year, but ownership hasn't changed."
- **Watch out:** Don't speculate. Return to the product.

### "Who owns Leica?"
- **Often behind it:** Curiosity
- **Say something like:** "The Kaufmann family holds a majority, and Blackstone holds a minority stake."

### "What did Leitz do during the war?"
- **Often behind it:** Serious history question
- **Say something like:** "It's an important question. Like much of German industry, Leitz produced for the military, and historians have documented forced labor. The Leitz family also helped Jewish employees escape, known as the Leica Freedom Train. Both are true."
- **Watch out:** Never minimize. Offer to point them to further reading.
