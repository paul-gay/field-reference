Topic| Explanation  
---|---  
35mm| The format Leica popularized; 24 x 36mm frames, typically 24 or 36 exposures per roll.  
120 (medium format)| Roll film for frames like 6 x 4.5, 6 x 6 and 6 x 7 cm.  
Large format| Sheet film, commonly 4 x 5 inches and up.  
Color negative| Most forgiving; processed in C-41 chemistry. Classic stocks include Kodak Portra and Ektar.  
Slide (reversal)| Positive transparencies with rich color; processed in E-6; much less exposure latitude.  
Black and white| Processed in many developers, often at home. Classic stocks include Kodak Tri-X and Ilford HP5 Plus.  
Push and pull| Rating film at a higher (push) or lower (pull) ISO and adjusting development.  
Latitude| Color negative film tolerates overexposure well; err toward more light.  
Scanning| Lab scans, flatbed scanners, or "camera scanning" with a digital camera and macro lens.  
Availability| Film stocks come and go and prices change often. Check current supply before recommending a specific stock.
