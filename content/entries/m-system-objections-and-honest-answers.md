They say| A strong, honest answer  
---|---  
"Manual focus seems hard."| "It feels strange for about five minutes. Try it here." Offer zone focusing, or show the M EV1's focus aids.  
"No autofocus at this price?"| "That's a deliberate choice. The M is about being in control and keeping the camera small and quiet. If you want autofocus with the Leica look, the Q3 might be the better fit, and I'm happy to show you."  
"Why not a mirrorless camera with an adapter?"| "You can, and some people do. What you give up is the rangefinder experience, the size, and a body designed around these lenses."  
"I'll wait for the next M."| "Leica hasn't announced one, so I'd never have you buy around a rumor. Your lenses will carry forward to any future M."  
"It's so expensive."| Talk about the long view: lenses that last decades, strong value retention, Leica Pre-Owned and trade in, financing, and extended warranty options.
