Service| What it is| When to offer it  
---|---|---  
Test Drive| Leica lets clients try a camera before buying.| A client torn between models, or nervous about manual focus.  
Trade In| Upgrade or switch systems by trading existing equipment toward new Leica gear.| Upgraders, and switchers from other brands.  
Financing| Pay monthly options.| When price is the only barrier. Offer it matter of factly, never as pressure.  
Leica Pre-Owned and the Classic Store| Certified secondhand Leica equipment through Leica.| Budget conscious clients, collectors, and film shooters.  
  
> **Confirm the details**
>
> Terms for test drives, trade in values, financing and warranties vary by country and change over time. Learn your store's current programs in week one.
