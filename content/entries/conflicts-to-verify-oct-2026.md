The Field Snapshots (October 2026) are the newer source, but a few details differ from the earlier guides and Snapshots. Check these against Leica's own spec sheets, then fix whichever entry is wrong.

| Topic | Field Snapshots say | Earlier guides say | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| M11 (standard) | Black and silver M11 ended April 2025; only the Glossy Black Paint M11 is still offered | Snapshot 04 presents the "Leica M11" as a current camera | Treat Snapshot 04 as describing the M11 platform. In store, sell the M11-P, Glossy Black Paint or EV1. |
| Q3 43 crop modes | 60, 75, 90 and 120mm | 60, 75, 90, 120 and 150mm (from reviews) | Check the camera menu on a demo unit. |
| Leitzphone launch | March 2026 | February 2026 | Minor; either way it isn't sold in the US. |
| Q3 stabilization | Every Q3 has a stabilized lens | The Hard Questions entry said only the Q3 43 and SL were stabilized | Corrected here to "the Q3 family and SL cameras are stabilized." Confirm on the spec sheet. |
| Q3 Monochrom autofocus | Autofocus without phase detection | Earlier guides didn't specify | Worth knowing for the Q3 vs Monochrom conversation. |
