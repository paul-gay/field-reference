> **Catchphrase**
>
> "This is the lens most people never take off: a 35mm Summicron, small enough to forget and good enough never to replace."

**Then pause.** Ask what they photograph. If the answer is 'a bit of everything,' this is the lens.

## The walkthrough
| Step | Say |
|---|---|
| Description | A compact 35mm f/2 lens, around 255 grams. |
| Inspiration | Leica has made 35mm Summicrons since the late 1950s; it's the classic street and documentary view. |
| Features | Wide enough for context, close enough for intimacy. The focus tab lets you focus by feel. |
| Style | Small, discreet, balanced. |
| Other | A great first lens; add a 75 or 90 later. |

## Adapt the opening
- **First M:** If you only buy one lens this year, I'd start here.
- **Street:** This is the street photographer's classic.
- **Traveler:** The whole kit fits in a small bag.

## Put in their hands
Mount it on an M, set f/8 and 3 meters, and let them shoot without focusing.

## Skip unless asked
Element counts and design history beyond one line.

## Scene: Nina, buying her first M
- **Nina:** Which lens should I start with?
- **You:** What do you photograph most?
- **Nina:** Everything, honestly.
- **You:** Then this is the lens most people never take off...
