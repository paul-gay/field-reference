## The Story
**The D-Lux line.** Leica's compact everyday cameras, developed with a partner and tuned with Leica's design and interface.

**2024.** The D-Lux 8 brought the clean, minimal Leica menu to the compact line.

**Its role.** Often the first Leica, and a travel companion for M and Q owners.

## The Craft
### Outer strengths
- Physical dials in a pocketable body.
- Minimal, Leica style menu.
- A classic compact silhouette.

### Inner strengths
- **Four Thirds sensor**, much larger than a phone's.
- **Vario-Summilux zoom**, 24 to 75mm equivalent, f/1.7 to 2.8.
- Leica color and design.

## In Hand
