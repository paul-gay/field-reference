summary: "Get them making pictures within 30 seconds."
---

* Turn the camera on and hand it over in full auto. "Just point it at something and press."
  * Show the image at full magnification on the screen.
  * Press the crop button to show 50mm and 75mm framing.
  * Twist the macro ring and photograph the leather strap texture, a watch dial, or their ring.
  * If they're torn between Q3 and Q3 43, have them photograph the same person from the same spot with both. The choice usually becomes obvious.

> **Pro move**
>
> Ask to photograph the client's companion with the Q3 43 in the store's best light. Seeing someone they love rendered beautifully is more persuasive than any spec.
