## The Story
**The night lens.** Noctilux means night light. Leica's first Noctilux, a 50mm f/1.2, arrived in 1966.

**January 2026.** The first 35mm Noctilux, the fastest 35 in Leica's history, and the first Noctilux that focuses close, down to 0.5m.

**Made in Wetzlar.** Designed and handcrafted in Wetzlar, optimized for high resolution M sensors.

## The Craft
### Outer strengths
- Compact for a Noctilux, with an extendable hood.
- A long focusing throw for precise focus at f/1.2.
- 11 aperture blades for round highlights.

### Inner strengths
- **Three aspherical elements** made in house by precision glass moulding, to tolerances of a few nanometers.
- **A floating element** keeps quality consistent close up.
- **10 elements in 5 groups.**
