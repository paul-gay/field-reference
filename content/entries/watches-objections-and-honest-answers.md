They say| Answer  
---|---  
"Why buy a watch from a camera company?"| "Leica's founder was a trained watchmaker, and these are built with specialist German and Swiss movement partners. It's the same precision instrument thinking."  
"How does it compare to an Omega or Breitling?"| Be generous about other brands. "Those are wonderful watches. This is something very few people wear, with complications you won't find elsewhere, like the push crown and aperture style power reserve."  
"Will it hold value?"| "I can't promise that for any watch. What I can say is that Leica has a deep collector community, and a ZM watch was offered at the Leitz Photographica Auction for the first time in 2025."  
"Is the movement in house?"| Be precise: ZM 1 and ZM 2 movements are developed with Lehmann Präzision; the automatics use Chronode calibres. Don't overclaim.
