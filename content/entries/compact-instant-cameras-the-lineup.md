Model| What it is| Who it suits  
---|---|---  
D-Lux 8| Compact with a Four Thirds sensor (about 17MP) and a Leica DC Vario-Summilux lens covering roughly 24 to 75mm equivalent, f/1.7 to 2.8. Minimal, Leica style controls and menu.| Travelers, families, and anyone who wants better photos than a phone in a pocketable camera with Leica design.  
Sofort 2| Hybrid instant camera. Shoots digitally, saves to a memory card, and prints chosen images on instant film. Can also print photos from a phone via the app.| Parties, weddings, travel, gifts, younger clients, and anyone who loves physical prints.  
  
> **Check before quoting**
>
> Confirm current US prices, film type and pack availability, and finishes in your store's system. Instant film is a consumable, so know what you stock.
