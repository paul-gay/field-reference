title: "What not to say: accuracy flags"
category: "Accuracy"

summary: "Trust is the product. One confident error undoes an hour of expertise."
---

Trap| Say this instead  
---|---  
"Leica Camera AG was founded in 1869."| The company's roots go back to 1849 and Leitz took over in 1869; the first Leica camera came in 1925, and Leica Camera AG as a company dates from 1996.  
"Leica makes microscopes too."| Leica Microsystems, Leica Geosystems and Leica Biosystems are separate companies with different owners that share the Leitz heritage.  
Promising an M12, Q4, or medium format S4| All are rumors. Rumor sites point to an M12 around 2027, but Leica has confirmed nothing.  
"This will go up in value."| Leica holds value unusually well and has a deep collector market; no individual camera is a guaranteed investment.  
"The company is being sold."| Sale reports surfaced in January 2026; ownership is unchanged.  
ZM watch calibre names| Leica's own sources are inconsistent on hand wound calibre names. The ZM 12 calibre is LA-3002, not LA-3001.  
Quoting prices from memory| Check the current US list. Tariffs and currency have moved prices.  
"Capa shot D Day on a Leica."| Photographer and camera attributions are often disputed. Stick to well documented ones like Cartier-Bresson and Korda.
