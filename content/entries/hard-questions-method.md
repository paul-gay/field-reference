## The four steps
| Step | What it means |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge | Show you heard it. "That's a fair question." |
| Ask | Find what's behind it. "What's making you hesitate?" |
| Answer | One short, honest answer. No lecture. |
| Act | Let them try it, see it, or decide. Then stop talking. |

## Rules that never bend
- **Never argue.** You can be right and still lose the client.
- **Never invent.** "Let me find out" is always acceptable.
- **Never talk another brand or product down.**
- **Never promise** prices, value, availability, allocation or unannounced products.
- **Follow store policy** on discounts, holds, returns and tax, exactly.
- **Silence is fine.** After an answer, let them think.
