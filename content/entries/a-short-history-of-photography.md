Year| Milestone  
---|---  
Ancient to 1500s| The camera obscura principle is described and used by artists.  
about 1826 to 1827| Nicéphore Niépce makes the oldest surviving camera photograph, _View from the Window at Le Gras_.  
1839| Louis Daguerre's daguerreotype process is announced publicly.  
1841| William Henry Fox Talbot patents the calotype, a negative and positive process allowing copies.  
1851| Wet plate collodion brings sharper, faster images.  
1870s| Dry gelatin plates make photography more practical.  
1888| Kodak's first camera: "You press the button, we do the rest."  
1900| The Kodak Brownie brings photography to the masses.  
1913 to 1925| Barnack's Ur-Leica, then the Leica I: the 35mm camera becomes practical.  
1935| Kodachrome color slide film.  
1948| Polaroid's first instant camera.  
1954| The Leica M3.  
1959| The Nikon F helps make the SLR the professional standard.  
1975| Steven Sasson at Kodak builds the first digital camera prototype.  
1985| Minolta Maxxum (Alpha) 7000 popularizes autofocus SLRs.  
late 1990s to 2000s| Digital SLRs take over professional work.  
2006 and 2009| Leica M8, then the full frame M9.  
2007| The iPhone begins the smartphone photography era.  
2008 onward| Mirrorless interchangeable lens cameras emerge and later dominate.  
2020s| Computational photography, AI subject detection, Content Credentials, and a film revival.  
2025| Leica celebrates 100 years of the Leica I.
