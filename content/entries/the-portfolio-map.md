## Cameras

Family| Current models| Who it's for and the one line to say  
---|---|---  
M digital| M11, M11-P, M11-D, M11 Monochrom| The purist. Rangefinder, manual focus, 60MP. M11-P adds Content Credentials and drops the red dot for discretion. M11-D has no rear screen, so it shoots like film. Monochrom shoots only black and white.  
M EV1| M EV1 (October 2025)| The first M with an integrated electronic viewfinder instead of a rangefinder. For clients who want M lenses and the M body but see better through a screen, including eyeglass wearers and close focus lovers.  
M film| M6, MP, M-A| The analog heart. M6 (reissued in 2022) and MP have light meters; M-A is fully mechanical with no battery at all.  
Q| Q3, Q3 43, Q3 Monochrom| The gateway Leica and often the best seller. Full frame 60MP sensor with a fixed lens and autofocus. Q3 uses a 28mm Summilux f/1.7; Q3 43 uses an APO-Summicron 43 f/2; Q3 Monochrom is the black and white version.  
SL| SL3, SL3-S, SL3-P| The professional L-Mount mirrorless system. SL3 is the 60MP resolution body; SL3-S is the 24MP speed and video body; SL3-P (June 2026, $6,690 body) is 44MP with 8K video, 40fps, hybrid AF, Content Credentials, and no red logo. SL3 Reporter is a rugged edition.  
Compact| D-Lux 8| A pocketable everyday camera with Leica design and interface.  
Instant| Sofort 2| Fun, social, giftable. A first Leica moment for a younger client.  
  
## Beyond cameras

Category| What to know  
---|---  
Mobile| Leica Leitzphone powered by Xiaomi (February 2026), Leica's first internationally available smartphone. The LUX app for iPhone (now with Artist Looks by Greg Williams and Steve McCurry, and iPhone 18 Pro support), LUX Grip, and LUX cases. Mobile is Leica's fastest growing division.  
Watches| Hand wound ZM 1 and ZM 2 (movements by Lehmann Präzision, Germany) and automatic ZM 11 and ZM 12 (movements by Chronode, Switzerland). ZM 1 and ZM 2 now come in Urban Green. Names follow a plain language formula rather than reference codes.  
Sport Optics| Noctivid, Ultravid (including the White Ocean edition for sailing), Trinovid, Geovid Max rangefinding binoculars (September 2026), spotting scopes, riflescopes, thermal imaging.  
Home cinema| Cine 1 laser TV, Cine Play 1, Cine Compact 1, with furniture integrations from USM and YOMEI.  
Eyecare| Leica spectacle lenses, sold through opticians.  
Pre-Owned| Leica Pre-Owned and the Leica Classic Store, plus trade in toward new equipment.  
  
> **Price discipline**
>
> Leica prices in the US have moved with tariffs and currency. Treat every number in this guide as a reference point and confirm the current US price list on your first day. Quoting an old price is the fastest way to lose a client's trust.
