## The one sentence version
Autofocus is the camera answering two questions: **which way** to move the lens, and **how far**. The systems differ in how quickly they can answer.

## The main systems
| System | How it works | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Contrast detection** | Moves the lens back and forth until the image is at its sharpest (highest contrast). | Very accurate; no calibration issues. | Has to "hunt" past focus to find it, so slower, and weaker with moving subjects or low contrast scenes. |
| **Phase detection (on sensor)** | Special pixels on the sensor compare light arriving from opposite sides of the lens. The difference tells the camera both direction and distance at once. | Fast and decisive; excellent for tracking moving subjects. | Can struggle with some patterns or very low light; needs good subject detection to shine. |
| **Hybrid** | Combines phase detection for speed with contrast detection for final precision, often helped by depth information. | Best of both: fast and accurate. | More complex; performance depends on the camera's processing. |
| **Dedicated module (DSLRs)** | A separate phase detection sensor below the mirror. | Fast in its day. | Can drift out of calibration; no longer the industry direction. |
| **Laser or depth sensors (phones)** | Measure distance with light or a depth map. | Quick at close range. | Mostly a phone technique. |

## Subject detection: the modern layer
On top of the focus system, recent cameras use trained recognition to find **eyes, faces, bodies, animals and vehicles** and keep focus on them as they move. This is what makes modern autofocus feel effortless: you frame, the camera follows.

## Which Leicas use what
| Camera | Focus system |
|---|---|
| M11 family, M EV1, film Ms | **Manual focus only.** Rangefinder on the M11 family and film Ms; EVF with peaking and magnification on the M EV1. |
| Q3, Q3 43 | **Hybrid** with phase detection and subject recognition. |
| Q3 Monochrom | Autofocus **without** the phase detection of the color Q3s. |
| SL3, SL3-S, SL3-P | **Hybrid phase detection** with subject recognition. The SL3-P's 819 point system recognizes people, animals and cars and is widely described as Leica's best AF yet. |
| D-Lux 8 | **Contrast** autofocus. |

> **Check before quoting**
>
> Exact AF point counts, detection types and menu names vary by model and firmware. Confirm on Leica's spec sheet or the camera itself.
