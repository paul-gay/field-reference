Marking| Where| What it tells you  
---|---|---  
Name and specs, for example **SUMMILUX-M 1:1.4/35 ASPH.**|  Front ring| Family, mount, maximum aperture as a ratio (1:1.4 means f/1.4), focal length in mm, and aspherical design.  
Maker and place, for example **LEICA CAMERA WETZLAR GERMANY**|  Front ring or barrel| Older lenses say ERNST LEITZ WETZLAR or LEITZ CANADA, which collectors use to date and value them.  
Serial number| Front ring or barrel| Dates the lens and tracks production; collectors check it against published serial lists.  
Filter size, for example **E39** or **E46**|  Front ring| Filter thread diameter in mm; E46 takes 46mm filters.  
Aperture ring| Barrel| f numbers with clicks, often in half stops.  
Distance scale| Focus ring| Focus distance in meters and feet.  
Depth of field scale| Between aperture and distance scales| Paired aperture numbers show what will be sharp; the key to zone focusing.  
Red dot| Near the mount| Align with the camera's red dot to mount the lens.  
Six bit code| On the bayonet flange| Black and white segments that tell a digital M which lens is attached.  
Focus tab| Under the barrel on many lenses| A fingertip lever for fast focusing by feel.  
Close focus detent| Focus ring, on newer lenses| A felt stop at 0.7m where rangefinder coupling ends.
