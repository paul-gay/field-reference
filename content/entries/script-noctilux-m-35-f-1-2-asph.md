> **Catchphrase**
>
> "This is the first 35mm Noctilux, the fastest 35 Leica has ever made, for the moments that happen after the light goes."

**Then pause.** Take them to the darkest spot in the store before saying anything else.

## The walkthrough
| Step | Say |
|---|---|
| Description | A 35mm f/1.2 that focuses down to half a meter. |
| Inspiration | Noctilux means night light; the first Noctilux arrived in 1966. This is the first at 35mm. |
| Features | Three aspherical elements made in Wetzlar by precision glass moulding, a floating element for close range, and a long focus throw for precise focusing. |
| Style | Cinematic and velvety. |
| Other | Easiest to focus wide open with the M EV1 or a Visoflex. |

## Adapt the opening
- **Collector:** A landmark: the first Noctilux at 35mm.
- **Event lover:** Dinners, concerts, city streets, no flash.
- **Noctilux 50 owner:** The same magic, with more of the scene.

## Put in their hands
Mount it on an M EV1, find the darkest corner, and focus on a face at f/1.2.

## Skip unless asked
Tolerance numbers unless they love engineering.

## Scene: Claire, who photographs dinners with friends
- **Claire:** Restaurants are always too dark.
- **You:** Come with me for a second. (darkest corner) Now look through here.
