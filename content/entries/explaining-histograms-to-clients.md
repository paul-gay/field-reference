## The 20 second version
> **Say it like this**
>
> "This graph is every tone in your photo, from black on the left to white on the right. If it's piled against either edge, you've lost detail there. It's more honest than the screen, because screens look different in sun and shade."

## A demo that clicks
1. On a Q3 or SL3, turn on the live histogram.
2. Point at a bright window and watch it pile up on the right.
3. Dial in −1 or −2 compensation and watch it slide away from the edge.
4. Turn on highlight warnings and show the window blinking, then stop blinking.

## When to bring it up
- **Beginners:** skip it on day one. Mention it at the Akademie workshop or the follow up.
- **Enthusiasts switching systems:** show where the histogram and clipping warnings live in the Leica menu. It reassures them.
- **Monochrom and film clients:** talk tones and the Zone System; they'll love it.
