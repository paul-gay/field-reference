* Photograph openly. Zone focusing makes you fast, not invisible, and it isn't a tool for being sneaky.  
  * If someone objects, smile, show them the picture if they ask, and delete it if they want.
  * Be especially thoughtful with children, people in vulnerable situations, and private moments.
  * Laws about photographing people in public vary by country and state. Know the rules where you shoot.
