## The L-Mount Alliance
Leica, Panasonic and Sigma founded it in 2018. It now also includes Leitz Cine, DJI, Astrodesign, Samyang, Blackmagic, Sirui, Viltrox and Freefly. One mount, a huge catalog of native AF lenses. It works both ways: a Lumix or Sigma owner can buy Leica SL glass today.

## Adapters
- **M-Adapter L, $480:** M lenses on any L-Mount body; reads coding, applies profiles, writes EXIF. Manual focus with peaking. Pitch to M owners: keep your M glass and add an SL for AF, video and telephoto.
- **R-Adapter L, $1,115:** Leica R lenses on L-Mount, with aperture and EXIF for 43 post 1976 R lenses.
- **Old CL and TL lenses:** APS-C TL lenses mount on SL bodies and shoot in crop mode. No current Leica APS-C camera.
