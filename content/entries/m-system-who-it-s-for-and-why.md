Client| Why the M fits  
---|---  
Street and documentary photographers| Small, quiet, discreet. You see outside the frame lines, so you can anticipate the moment.  
Film shooters, returning or new| Leica still builds film M cameras, and every M lens works on both film and digital bodies.  
Q owners ready for more| They love the Leica look and want interchangeable lenses and more intention.  
Collectors| Editions, heritage, and a system with 70 years of lenses and history.  
People who want to slow down| The rangefinder forces attention. Many clients describe it as making photography feel meaningful again.  
  
## Who it's probably not for

  * Someone who mainly shoots fast sports or wildlife. Point them to the SL.
  * Someone who wants autofocus and doesn't want to learn manual focus. Point them to the Q, or to the M EV1 if they insist on M lenses and want easier focusing aids.
  * Someone buying a camera mainly for video. The SL is the right tool.

Saying this out loud builds trust. Clients remember the advisor who steered them to the right camera, even when it was cheaper.
