Factor| Effect on the zone| Rule of thumb  
---|---|---  
Aperture| Smaller opening (bigger f number) makes the zone deeper.| f/8 is the street standard. f/11 for more margin. f/2 makes the zone razor thin.  
Focal length| Wider lenses give deeper zones.| 28mm is very forgiving, 35mm is the classic, 50mm is noticeably thinner, 90mm is barely practical.  
Focus distance| Focusing farther away makes the zone deeper.| At normal distances, the zone extends roughly one third in front of the focus point and two thirds behind.  
Standard of sharpness| "Acceptably sharp" depends on how big you view the image.| Scales are designed for normal prints. On a 60MP file viewed at 100%, the zone edges look softer, so be conservative.  
  
> **The high resolution tip**
>
> On an M11 or M EV1, many photographers read the depth of field scale one stop wider than the aperture they actually set. Shoot at f/8, but use the f/5.6 marks to judge the zone. The result looks crisp even at full magnification.
