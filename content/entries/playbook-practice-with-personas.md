## The exercise
Write a short profile of an imagined client: age, work, passions, style, what they need. Then present the same product to two very different personas and notice how the arguments change.

## A Leica persona to try
**Clara, 30.** A scriptwriter who loves black and white cinema and develops her own film in a darkroom. Her style mixes the 1950s with today. She wants something durable she never has to put away.

- What would you show her first?
- What's your catchphrase?
- Which features matter to her, and which would you skip?

*(Possible direction: a Monochrom or an M6, framed around black and white cinema and craft.)*

## Make it a habit
Write one new persona a week based on real visitors, and add it here.
