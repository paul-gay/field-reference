## The Story
**A focal length for storytelling.** 35mm takes in a person and their world together. It's the classic view of street and documentary photographers, wide enough for context, close enough for intimacy.

**A long Summicron lineage.** Leica has made 35mm Summicrons since the late 1950s, and early versions are prized by collectors today.

**Today.** The current ASPH. version keeps the famous compact size while using aspherical design for modern sharpness across the frame.

## The Craft
### Outer strengths
- Very compact and light, so the M stays small and discreet.
- Focus tab for fast focusing by feel.
- Crisp half stop aperture clicks.

### Inner strengths
- **Aspherical design** for sharpness into the corners without extra size.
- **f/2**: the Summicron balance of speed, size and sharpness.
- **6 bit coding** so digital Ms recognize it.
