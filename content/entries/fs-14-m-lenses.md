| Name | Max aperture | Think of it as |
|---|---|---|
| Noctilux | f/0.95 to f/1.25 | The night lens. Flagship. |
| Summilux | f/1.4 | Fast, low light, shallow focus. |
| Summicron | f/2 | The core workhorse. |
| Elmarit | f/2.8 | Small and light. |
| Elmar / Super-Elmar | f/3.4 to f/4 | Compact, wide or macro. |
| Summaron | f/5.6 | Classic reissue, tiny. |
| APO prefix | | Top correction, no color fringes. |
| ASPH. suffix | | Aspherical elements, sharp wide open. |
| Classic Line | | Historic designs, softer glow. |

The f/2.4 Summarit line is no longer made; customers may find them used.

## Price tiers
- **Entry, ~$3,100 to $4,500:** Elmarit-M 28, Summicron-M 50, Summaron-M 28, Summicron-M 35.
- **Summilux, ~$4,600 to $8,600:** including the Classic Line 35 and 50 f/1.4 reissues.
- **APO-Summicron, ~$5,300 to $10,000:** 35, 50, 75 and 90.
- **Noctilux, ~$8,900 to $15,600:** 35 f/1.2, 50 f/0.95, 50 f/1.2 Classic, 75 f/1.25.

## First lens by customer
Street, travel, all round: 35mm. Portraits, classic normal view: 50mm. Tight budget: Elmarit-M 28 or pre owned. Loves vintage character: Classic Line. Wants the ultimate: APO 50 or a Noctilux.

## New and notable
- **Summicron-M 66 f/2 (Sept 2026):** limited to 660 numbered pieces, $10,900. Based on a Cold War era Mandler design. Its aperture ring turns the opposite way.
- Safari and glossy black editions of several Summilux and Summicron lenses.

## Explaining the look
Lead with micro contrast and how the subject separates. Show the out of focus areas wide open on a face. Modern ASPH and APO for precision; Classic Line for glow. Say "Leica Germany" unless the box says Wetzlar.
