Leica began producing binoculars in 1907, nearly two decades before the Leica I camera. Today Leica Sport Optics makes binoculars, monoculars, spotting scopes, laser rangefinders, rangefinding binoculars, riflescopes and thermal imaging devices for nature observation, travel, sailing, birding and hunting. In fiscal 2025/26 Leica reported stable demand in this division.

> **Why it belongs in a camera store**
>
> It's the same optical expertise: glass, coatings, contrast and color fidelity. A client who values what a Summilux does for a photograph often values what a Noctivid does for an evening of birdwatching.
