## The technique
Put the client back at the center. Before describing a product's style, notice:
- What are they wearing today?
- What's their style and attitude?
- How does the product resemble them?

Then choose adjectives that fit both the brand's voice and the person. For example, casual to dressy, rugged yet polished, relaxed, elegant, understated.

## Why it works
People buy things that feel like an extension of themselves. When your description mirrors them, the product already feels like theirs.

## How it transfers to Leica
- A client in understated, all black clothing: the M11-P or a Monochrom, "discreet, nothing to announce itself."
- A client with a vintage, analog style: the M6 or a Classic Line lens, "timeless, made to be used for decades."
- A client with bright, playful energy: the Sofort 2, "pure fun you can hand to someone."
