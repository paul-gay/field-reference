> **Your fifteen second intro**
>
> "This is a full frame Leica with the lens built in. It focuses for you, it's stabilized, it's sealed against dust and rain, and it shoots 8K video. One camera, one lens, nothing to decide before you leave the house."

**Every Q3 shares:** 60MP full frame sensor with Triple Resolution and crop modes, stabilized fixed lens, 5.76M dot OLED EVF, tilting touchscreen, IP52, 8K/30p and 4K/60p video, SD card, USB-C, FOTOS.

## Ask first
- Do you want one camera that does it all?
- More places and scenes, or more people?
- Do you shoot any video?
- What are you carrying now?

## Show them
- Twist the macro ring and focus close on their watch dial.
- Step through the crop modes: 28, 35, 50, 75.
- Shoot at waist height with the tilting screen.
- Grab an eye with subject detection.

## If they say
- **"I can't change the lens."** That's what keeps it compact and sealed. The crop modes give you several framings, and the 60MP file holds up when you crop.
- **"Q3 or Q3 43?"** 28 for places and the life around you. 43 is closer to your eye and lovely for people. Hold both and look through each.
- **"Why not an M?"** If you want autofocus, sealing and video with no lens decisions, the Q. If you want the rangefinder and a lens system, the M.

## Pair it with
Spare battery and fast SD card, thumb support, wrist or neck strap, protector or half case, Akademie Product Essentials.

> **The bridge sale**
>
> Many Q owners come back for an M within a year or two. Sell the Q well, explain the M honestly, and you've started a relationship.
