## Shared vocabulary
- **Triple Resolution:** 60MP bodies can also record 36MP or 18MP DNG files. Same full sensor, smaller files.
- **Content Credentials:** tamper evident proof of where and how an image was made. On M11-P, M11-D, M EV1, Q3 Monochrom, SL3-S, SL3-P.
- **Metal Gray:** new finish from May 2026 on M11-P, Q3 and D-Lux 8.
- **APO:** red, green and blue light focus at the same point. No color fringing, top sharpness.
- **Monochrom:** a sensor that only sees black and white, for more detail and cleaner high ISO.
