Distance| Everyday reference  
---|---  
2 m / 6 ft| A person lying down; conversation distance; the height of a doorway.  
3 m / 10 ft| Across a small living room; about four normal steps; a basketball hoop's height.  
5 m / 16 ft| Slightly longer than a car.  
10 m / 33 ft| Across a two lane street, roughly.  
  
## Drills

  * **Pace it:** an adult step is roughly 0.75 m (2.5 ft). Pace out 3 m a dozen times until it's automatic.
  * **Guess and check:** guess the distance to a sign or tree, then focus on it with the rangefinder and read the scale. Do ten a day for a week.
  * **Frame the gap:** learn what a person looks like in your viewfinder at 2, 3 and 5 m. Their size in the frame becomes your rangefinder.
