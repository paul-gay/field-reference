* Hand over an SL3-P with the 24 to 70mm f/2.8 zoom and show the eye tracking autofocus on the client or their companion.
  * Switch between photo and video modes to show the separate, color coded interfaces.
  * Fire a short burst to demonstrate speed.
  * Show how few buttons there are, then how they can be customized.
  * If the client owns M lenses, mount one with the adapter and show focus peaking.

> **Pro move**
>
> Ask a professional client to bring their own memory card and shoot a few frames. Let them take the files home and edit them in their own workflow. Pros buy on results.
