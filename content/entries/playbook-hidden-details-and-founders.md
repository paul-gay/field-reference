## 1. The hidden detail
Show the client something small they'd never notice alone, like a symbol engraved where only the owner sees it. Discovering it with you makes it personal, and they'll show it to friends.

## 2. The founder's voice
A short, human quote from a founder explaining a design choice is more memorable than any spec. It turns a feature into a decision someone made for a reason.

## 3. The cultural moment
When a famous person or film made the product iconic, that moment gives it meaning beyond function.

## How it transfers to Leica
| Story type | Leica example |
|---|---|
| Hidden detail | The engraving and serial number on an M; the lens code on the bayonet; the depth of field scale few people read |
| Founder's voice | Ernst Leitz II's 1924 decision, usually quoted as "It will be risked" |
| Cultural moment | Cartier-Bresson and the decisive moment; Korda's portrait of Che Guevara |

> **Keep it accurate**
>
> Only tell founder quotes and cultural stories you can stand behind. If an attribution is debated, say so.
