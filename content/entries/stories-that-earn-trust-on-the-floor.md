## The Leica Freedom Train

In the 1930s, Ernst Leitz II quietly helped Jewish employees, their families and others in Wetzlar leave Nazi Germany by assigning them to Leitz sales offices abroad, including in New York, often with a Leica and support until they found work. The effort continued until the war made it impossible. Leitz was also politically at risk himself as an opponent of the regime. Accounts vary on the number of people helped, so don't quote a figure; tell it as a story about character. Use it with care: it's a moral story, never a sales tactic, and it lands best when a client asks why the brand matters to people.

## The pictures that made the legend

  * **Henri Cartier-Bresson** built "the decisive moment" around a Leica and a 50mm lens. He's the patron saint of the M.
  * **Alberto Korda** 's 1960 portrait of Che Guevara, _Guerrillero Heroico_ , is usually credited to a Leica M2 with a 90mm lens.
  * **Alfred Eisenstaedt** 's V J Day kiss in Times Square (1945) was made on a Leica.
  * The Soviet flag raised over the Reichstag in 1945 was photographed with a Leica.

Leica's own campaign line for this heritage is "The World Deserves Witnesses." That's the emotional argument: the camera is a tool for bearing witness, not a gadget.

## Proof of enduring value: the auction house

Leica owns Leitz Photographica Auction in Wetzlar, which holds sales twice a year (mid June and late November) and draws bidders from over 100 countries. Know three results cold:

Lot| Result| Why it matters  
---|---|---  
0 Series No. 105 (2022)| €14.4 million| Barnack's personal camera; world record for any camera.  
0 Series No. 112 (2025)| €7.2 million| Estimated at €800,000; sold during the centenary.  
M3 No. 700000 (2025)| €3.36 million| The first production M camera ever made.  
  
> **How to use it honestly**
>
> Never promise a client that a camera will appreciate. The honest point is narrower and stronger: Leica products hold value unusually well, there's a deep secondhand and collector market, and Leica runs its own Pre-Owned program and a Classic Store. Buying a Leica is buying into an ecosystem that doesn't expire.

## The culture layer

Leica runs roughly 30 Leica Galleries worldwide (the network turns 50 in 2026, with the anniversary exhibition _Personal Perspectives_), the Leica Akademie workshops, LFI magazine, the Leica Oskar Barnack Award, and the Leica Hall of Fame Award. In the store, these are reasons to invite a client back that have nothing to do with selling them something today.
