> **20 seconds**
>
> "The SL is our professional system. Autofocus, stabilization, 8K video, weather sealing, and Leica lenses, in a body designed to be simple. It's for photographers who need a camera to perform, every time."

> **Two minutes**
>
> "Professional cameras tend to be covered in buttons and menus. Leica took the opposite approach: a solid, minimalist body with a small set of controls you can customize, and a menu that's refreshingly simple. Underneath, it's a serious tool. The SL3-P, our newest, has 44 megapixels, 8K video, 40 frames a second with autofocus, and a new hybrid autofocus system.
>
> It's also an open system. The L-Mount is shared with Panasonic, Sigma and others, so you have Leica's own lenses plus a wide range of partner lenses and video gear. And if you own M lenses, you can adapt them."
