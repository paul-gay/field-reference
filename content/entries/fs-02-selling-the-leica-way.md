## The six step flow
| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1. Welcome | Greet, then get a camera into their hands early. Weight and sound sell before words do. |
| 2. Discover | What do you photograph? What do you use now? What frustrates you about it? |
| 3. Match | One body and one lens. Say why in one sentence that uses their words. |
| 4. Experience | Shoot in the store. Show the file big on a screen or their phone. |
| 5. Build | Lens, battery, strap, Akademie, trade in, financing, membership. |
| 6. Follow up | Ask for their first photos. Invite them to the next event. |

## Discovery questions that work
- **"What made you come in today?"** Gets the real trigger: a trip, a birthday, a new baby, a camera that broke.
- **"Show me a photo you love that you took."** Tells you their subject, focal length and taste in seconds.
- **"What do you carry now?"** A Sony or Fuji user hears different words than a phone only shooter.
- **"Where will this camera live?"** Bag, pocket, desk, studio. It decides Q vs M vs SL vs D-Lux.
- **"Is this for you or a gift?"** Changes everything about the conversation.

## Language that fits the brand
| Instead of | Try |
|---|---|
| "It's the best camera." | "It's the one that fits how you shoot." |
| "It's expensive because it's Leica." | "It's built by hand and built to last decades." |
| "Sharp lens." | "Look at the micro contrast and how the subject separates." |
| "Do you want to buy it?" | "Which finish feels like yours?" |

## The services menu
| Service | Note |
|---|---|
| Test Drive | Loaner program so they can live with it first. |
| Trade In | Credit toward new gear, plus periodic Trade In Days. Terms vary by store. |
| Financing | Pay monthly options. Lead with it when price is the only hesitation. |
| Extended warranty | Offer on every body sale. |
| Leica Akademie | Workshops in DC and other cities. One on one lessons from $499.99. |
| Leica Society Intl. | One year digital membership included with an M, SL or Q camera. |
| Customer Care | Repair, maintenance, M system service, Service Certificate. |
| Pre owned | Some Leica Stores carry Certified Pre Owned. Confirm your store's terms. |
| Registration | Help them register the product to their Leica Account at the counter. |

> **From watches to cameras**
>
> A customer choosing a Leica is buying an instrument, a story and a relationship, just like a Breitling or Omega buyer. The difference: a camera buyer comes back for lenses. Treat every sale as the first of several.
