Scenario| Lens| Starting settings and technique  
---|---|---  
Street photography in daylight| Summicron-M 35 f/2 or Summaron-M 28 f/5.6| f/8, zone focus at about 3m (35mm) so roughly 2 to 7m is sharp; Auto ISO; shutter 1/250 or faster. Pre focus near a doorway or pool of light and wait.  
Street at night| Summilux-M 35 or 28 f/1.4| f/1.4 to f/2, ISO up to 3200 or higher, 1/60 to 1/125. Focus with the rangefinder on faces lit by windows or signs.  
Travel with one lens| Summilux-M 35 f/1.4 or Summicron-M 35 f/2| f/5.6 to f/8 for scenes, f/2 for food and people at dinner. One lens keeps the bag small.  
Portrait of a partner or child| Summilux-M 50 or APO-Summicron-M 75| f/2 to f/2.8 for separation with some focus margin, focus on the nearest eye, keep the background a few meters behind the subject.  
Restaurant, candlelight, bars| Noctilux-M 50 f/0.95 or 35 f/1.2| f/1.2 to f/1.4, focus carefully on the eye (EVF helps), ISO as needed. The glow is the point.  
Architecture and interiors| Super-Elmar-M 21 or Summilux-M 24| f/5.6 to f/8, keep the camera level to avoid converging lines, use live view or the M EV1 to frame precisely.  
Landscape| Super-Elmar-M 21, Elmarit-M 28 or APO-Summicron-M 90| f/8, hyperfocal focusing for wides, base ISO, tripod for low light. The 90 picks out shapes in distant hills.  
Family life at home| Summicron-M 28 or Summilux-M 35 (close focus versions)| f/2 to f/2.8, Auto ISO, window light. Close focus to 0.4m catches small hands and details.  
Black and white on a Monochrom| Classic Line Summilux-M 50 or Summaron-M 28| Wide open for glow, stopped down for grit. A red filter darkens skies dramatically.  
Film on an M6 or MP| Summicron-M 35 or 50| ISO 400 film, sunny 16 as a check, f/8 and 1/250 in daylight. Small, classic, balanced on film bodies.
