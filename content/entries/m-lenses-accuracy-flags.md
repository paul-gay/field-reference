* Rangefinder coupling on traditional M lenses ends at 0.7m; close focus beyond that needs live view or an EVF.
  * No frame lines exist for 21mm or 24mm.
  * The Noctilux-M 35 is f/1.2 and launched in 2026; the 50 is f/0.95; the 75 is f/1.25.
  * There are two current Summilux-M 50 lenses: the ASPH. and the Classic Line version. Don't confuse them.
  * Confirm current catalog, stock and US prices before quoting; this guide avoids prices on purpose.
