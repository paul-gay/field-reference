> **Your fifteen second intro**
>
> "The SL is Leica's full frame mirrorless system. Fast autofocus, stabilization, serious video, weather sealing. It uses the L-Mount, so Leica, Sigma and Panasonic lenses all fit natively, and your M lenses adapt with one ring."

**Every SL3 shares:** L-Mount, in body stabilization, 5.76M dot EVF, 3.2" tilting touchscreen, IP54, phase detect hybrid AF, FOTOS.

**The simple sort:** resolution first, SL3. Speed and video first, SL3-S. Both in one body, SL3-P.

## Ask first
Stills, video, or both? What system are you coming from? Moving or still subjects? Do you own M lenses?

## Show them
- Track a colleague walking toward the SL3-P with eye detection on.
- Mount an M lens with the M-Adapter L and turn on peaking.
- Hand them the body and let the all metal build do the talking.

## If they say
- **"My Sony or Canon focuses faster."** Be honest: they're very good. The SL3-P is Leica's best AF so far. What you gain here is the build, the color, Leica glass, and a bridge to M lenses.
- **"Leica lenses are too expensive."** L-Mount means Sigma and Panasonic lenses fit natively. And the Summicron-SL 50 starts at $2,350.

## Pair it with
Vario-Elmarit-SL 24–70 f/2.8, Summicron-SL 35 or 50, M-Adapter L ($480), fast cards, SSD for video, spare battery.
