Days| Exercise  
---|---  
1 to 2| Distance guessing drills, no camera. Ten guesses each day, checked by pacing.  
3 to 4| 35mm, f/8, 3 m. Walk a busy street and shoot 100 frames without touching focus. Review which frames were sharp and why.  
5 to 6| Pick a stage: one spot with good light. Wait for 20 subjects to enter your zone.  
7 to 8| Switch between near (2 m) and far (5 m) presets by feel.  
9 to 10| Try 28mm and 50mm. Notice how the zone changes.  
11 to 12| Hyperfocal landscapes and cityscapes.  
13 to 14| A small story: 12 photographs on one theme, all zone focused. Print your favorite.
