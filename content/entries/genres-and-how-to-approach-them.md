Genre| Typical focal lengths| Starting settings| Leica fit  
---|---|---|---  
Street| 28 to 50mm| f/8, 1/250+, Auto ISO, zone focus| M with 28 or 35; Q3.  
Portrait| 50 to 90mm| f/1.4 to f/2.8, focus on the eye| M with 50 or 75; Q3 43; SL3-P.  
Wedding and events| 24 to 90mm| f/1.4 to f/2.8, silent shutter, AF tracking| SL3-P with zooms; M for a second, discreet angle.  
Landscape| 16 to 35mm and telephoto details| f/8 to f/11, base ISO, tripod, hyperfocal| SL3 (60MP); M with 21 or 28.  
Travel| 28 to 50mm| Versatile, light kit| Q3; M with one lens; D-Lux 8.  
Architecture and interiors| 16 to 28mm| f/8, level camera, tripod| SL with wide zooms; M with 21 or 24 and an EVF.  
Sports and wildlife| 100 to 600mm| 1/1000+, continuous AF| SL3-P; honest note: other systems offer longer lens catalogs.  
Macro| 90 to 100mm macro| f/8 to f/16, focus stacking, stable support| SL with the APO-Macro-Elmarit-SL 100 when available.  
Documentary| 28 to 50mm| Unobtrusive, reliable| M and Q, the classic tools of the genre.  
Astrophotography| 14 to 24mm, fast| Wide open, 15 to 25 seconds, high ISO, tripod| Possible, but not a Leica specialty.
