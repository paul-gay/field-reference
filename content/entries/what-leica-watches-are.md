Leica makes mechanical watches in two families. The ZM 1 and ZM 2 are hand wound watches with a patented push crown and camera inspired details. The ZM 11 and ZM 12 are automatic sports watches with interchangeable straps. They are sold in Leica Stores alongside cameras, and in fiscal 2025/26 Leica reported positive development in its watches and accessories business.

> **The origin story**
>
> Ernst Leitz I trained in watchmaking in Neuchâtel before he took over the Optical Institute in Wetzlar. Precision mechanics are the company's first trade. Leica watches are a return to that root, not a licensing exercise.
