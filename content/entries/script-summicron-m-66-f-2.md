> **Catchphrase**
>
> "Only about 200 of the originals were made, all for the US Navy. This is the first time photographers can own the idea."

**Then pause.** Let the story land. Collectors will ask the next question themselves.

## The walkthrough
| Step | Say |
|---|---|
| Description | A 66mm f/2 from the Classic Line, limited to 660 lenses worldwide. |
| Inspiration | Dr Walter Mandler designed the original Elcan 66 at Ernst Leitz Canada in the late 1960s for covert reconnaissance. |
| Features | High contrast with soft bokeh wide open, maximum detail stopped down, and a natural view between 50 and 75. |
| Style | Historic engravings; each lens individually numbered. |
| Other | The aperture ring turns the opposite way from other M lenses. Mention it before they shoot. |

## Adapt the opening
- **Collector:** Each one has its own number and a certificate.
- **Portrait photographer:** A focal length no one else has.
- **History lover:** Let me show you the engraving that says 60.

## Put in their hands
Point out the '60' engraving: it means 66.0mm, measured to a tenth of a millimeter.

## Skip unless asked
Allocation promises you can't confirm.

## Scene: Robert, a longtime Leica collector
- **Robert:** I've read about the Elcan for years.
- **You:** Only about 200 of the originals were made...
