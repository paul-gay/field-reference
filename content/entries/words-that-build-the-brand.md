Instead of| Say| Why  
---|---|---  
"Can I help you?"| "Welcome in. Have you been to a Leica Store before?"| Opens a conversation instead of inviting "just looking."  
"It's expensive because..."| "Here's what goes into it."| Explains value without apologizing for price.  
"It's a great investment."| "Leica cameras hold their value well, and they're made to be used for decades."| Accurate, and avoids a promise you can't keep.  
"Our cheapest camera"| "The most accessible way into Leica."| Keeps the entry point aspirational.  
"Deal," "discount," "sale"| "Kit," "offer," "program"| Luxury brands rarely use discount language.  
Megapixels first| What the picture will look like, and how it feels to use.| Most visitors care about experience before specs.  
"You should buy..."| "For what you describe, I'd look at..."| Advises rather than pushes.  
Jargon to a beginner| Plain words, then the term: "how blurry the background gets, which photographers call bokeh."| Teaches without making anyone feel small.  
  
## Words that carry the brand

Handmade in Wetzlar. Built to last decades. Designed around the essential. A camera you actually carry. A hundred years of photography. The world deserves witnesses.
