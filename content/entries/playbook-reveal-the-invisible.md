## The idea
Like describing a painting, don't narrate what's obvious. Describe what the client **can't** see and what it means for them, so they can picture owning it.

## Feature, advantage, benefit
For each technical point, walk it through three steps and add a small anecdote:
- **Feature:** what it is.
- **Advantage:** what it does better.
- **Benefit:** what it changes in the client's day.

## Keep it human
Expertise isn't using complicated words. It's adapting to the person in front of you and making the product accessible.

> **Instead of**
>
> Counting components in a movement, say what it means: "It's automatic, so you never have to remember to wind it, and it comes with a long warranty."

## How it transfers to Leica
