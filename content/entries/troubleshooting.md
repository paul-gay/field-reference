Problem| Likely cause| Fix  
---|---|---  
Everything slightly soft| Aperture too wide, or viewing at 100% on a high resolution file.| Use f/8 or f/11; read the scale one stop wider.  
Near subjects soft| They were closer than the zone's front edge.| Step back, or set a nearer zone.  
Far background soft| Zone set too close.| Move focus farther, or use hyperfocal.  
Motion blur| Shutter too slow.| Set a minimum of 1/250 in Auto ISO.  
Photos too dark at night| Small aperture in low light.| Raise ISO, open to f/5.6, or switch to focusing each shot.  
Zone feels unpredictable| Distance guessing still developing.| Repeat the pacing and guess and check drills.
