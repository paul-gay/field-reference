| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Snow or white walls look gray | Meter making bright scenes middle gray | +1 to +2 compensation |
| Faces dark against a bright window | Backlight fooling multi field metering | Spot meter the face, or +1 to +2 |
| Stage performer is a white blob | Dark stage fooling the meter | Spot or highlight weighted metering, or −1 to −2 |
| Photo looks fine on screen, too dark at home | Screen viewed in a dim room | Trust the histogram, not the screen |
| Photo looks too dark outdoors on the screen | Bright sun washing out the screen | Check the histogram or use the EVF |
| Sky blown out, foreground fine | Scene exceeds dynamic range | Protect highlights and lift shadows, use a graduated ND, or bracket |
| Exposure jumps between similar shots | Small framing changes swinging multi field | Use AE lock or manual exposure |
| Noisy shadows at high ISO | Underexposure | Expose brighter (without clipping) and darken later |
| Film negatives thin and grainy | Underexposed film | Give color negative film more light; meter for the shadows |
| One color has no detail (red flower, blue sky) | A single channel clipping | Check the RGB histogram; reduce exposure slightly |
