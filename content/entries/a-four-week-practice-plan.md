Week| Focus| Exercise  
---|---|---  
1| Rangefinder focusing| Shoot 100 frames of stationary subjects at f/2 and check sharpness at full magnification.  
2| Zone focusing| Set 35mm, f/8, 3 meters. Walk a busy street and shoot without touching the focus ring.  
3| Light and exposure| Shoot manual exposure using sunny 16, then compare to the meter.  
4| One lens, one theme| Make a set of 12 photos on one subject with one lens. Print your favorite and keep it at the counter.  
  
> **Why the print matters**
>
> A print you made with an M, displayed near the demo counter, gives every client conversation a real starting point: "I made this last month, here's how."
