Visitor| Signals| A good opening| What a win looks like  
---|---|---|---  
The curious passerby| Drifts in slowly, looks at the space more than the cases.| "Welcome in. Have you been in a Leica Store before?"| One story, one camera in hand, a smile on the way out.  
The phone photographer| Takes photos of the store with their phone, asks "what's the difference?"| "What do you shoot most with your phone?"| They see what a dedicated camera changes. Mention the LUX app as a bridge.  
The knowledgeable photographer| Goes straight to lenses, uses focal lengths and model names.| "What are you shooting with right now?"| A real conversation between peers. Let them teach you something.  
The dreamer| Often younger, lingers at the M cases, says "someday."| "Want to hold one? It's the best way to understand it."| They leave feeling welcomed, not priced out. Invite them to an event.  
The gifter| Asks what's popular, mentions a partner or parent.| "Tell me about who it's for. What do they love doing?"| A thoughtful match, which might be Sofort 2, a Q3, a ZM watch or leather goods.  
The collector| Asks about editions, serial numbers, availability.| "Are you adding to a collection? What's in it now?"| Trust, and a place on your client list.  
The tourist| Luggage, maps, mentions home country.| "Where are you visiting from?"| A memorable brand moment. Many have Leica Stores at home.  
The companion| Came with someone else, stands to the side.| Include them: "Are you a photographer too?"| They feel included. Companions often influence the decision.  
  
> **A rule worth keeping**
>
> Never judge buying power by appearance. Luxury retail is full of stories about the person in running shoes who bought the most expensive item in the store. More importantly, everyone deserves the same welcome.
