**Common speeds:** 1/8000, 1/4000, 1/2000, 1/1000, 1/500, 1/250, 1/125, 1/60, 1/30, 1/15, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1 second, and longer. "B" (bulb) holds the shutter open as long as you press.  
  
Speed| Typical use  
---|---  
1/1000 and faster| Sports, birds in flight, splashing water.  
1/250 to 1/500| Walking people, street, children.  
1/60 to 1/125| Everyday handheld with steady subjects.  
1/15 to 1/30| Handheld with stabilization or a steady hand; intentional motion blur.  
1 second and longer| Tripod: night scenes, silky water, light trails.  
Concept| Explanation  
---|---  
Handholding rule| Start at 1 over the focal length (1/50 for a 50mm), then faster for high resolution sensors.  
Stabilization| In body (IBIS) moves the sensor to counter shake; optical (OIS) moves a lens element. Neither freezes a moving subject.  
Focal plane shutter| Curtains travel across the sensor. Used in most interchangeable lens cameras, including the M and SL.  
Leaf shutter| Blades in the lens open from the center. Quiet, and syncs with flash at high speeds. Used in the Q3.  
Electronic shutter| The sensor reads out without moving parts. Silent, but fast motion can show rolling shutter distortion on slower readout sensors.  
Flash sync speed| The fastest speed at which a focal plane shutter is fully open for flash. Leaf shutters sync much faster.
