> **Catchphrase**
>
> "This is the M for people who want to see exactly what the sensor sees. Same M lenses, same craft, with a viewfinder that shows you the picture before you take it."

**Then pause.** Ask how the rangefinder felt, if they've tried one. Their answer tells you which M to show.

## The walkthrough
| Step | Say |
|---|---|
| Description | An M with a high resolution electronic viewfinder instead of a rangefinder. |
| Inspiration | In 1959 Leica made an M without a rangefinder, the M1. This brings that idea back for today. |
| Features | Focus peaking outlines what's sharp and magnification zooms in, so very fast and close focusing lenses are easy. Exact framing with any focal length. |
| Style | Pure M design. |
| Other | Confirm current availability before promising a date. |

## Adapt the opening
- **Glasses wearer:** Many people who wear glasses find this much easier than a rangefinder.
- **Noctilux lover:** At f/1.2 this makes focusing effortless.
- **M purist:** It's not replacing the rangefinder. It's another way to use the same lenses.

## Put in their hands
Mount a Summilux or Noctilux, turn on peaking, and let them focus on a face.

## Skip unless asked
Viewfinder dot counts unless they ask; it's the experience that sells.

## Scene: Ellen, who loved the M11 but struggled with the focusing patch
- **Ellen:** I love the M, but I just can't see the patch well.
- **You:** Then this is your M. Look through here.
- **Ellen:** Everything sharp is outlined in red!
- **You:** Same lenses, same camera. You just see it differently.
