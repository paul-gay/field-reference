Topic| Explanation  
---|---  
Raw| Unprocessed sensor data. Maximum editing flexibility. Leica cameras record raw as DNG, Adobe's open format.  
JPEG and HEIF| Processed, compressed files ready to share. Less latitude for editing.  
Color space| The range of colors a file can hold: sRGB (web standard), Adobe RGB (wider, for print), Display P3 (modern screens).  
Profiles and looks| Color and tone recipes applied to images; Leica Looks on the Q3 and SL3 families, and Artist Looks in the LUX app.  
Non destructive editing| Edits stored as instructions, leaving the original untouched (Lightroom, Capture One).  
Basic workflow| Import and back up, select, adjust exposure and white balance, refine contrast and color, crop, sharpen for output, export.  
Backups| The 3 2 1 rule: three copies, two types of media, one off site.  
Monitor calibration| A calibrated screen ensures what you edit matches what prints.  
Content Credentials| Tamper evident metadata showing an image's origin and edits; built into several Leica cameras.
