Binoculars are named like **8x42**. The first number is magnification: objects look 8 times closer. The second is the diameter of each front lens in millimeters: bigger means more light and a brighter view, but more size and weight.  
  
Concept| Plain explanation  
---|---  
8x versus 10x| 8x gives a wider, steadier view and is easier to hold. 10x brings things closer but shows more hand shake.  
Exit pupil| Front lens size divided by magnification. 8x42 gives about 5.25mm, bright enough for dusk. Bigger numbers help in low light.  
Field of view| How wide a scene you see. Wider makes finding moving birds easier.  
Close focus| How near you can focus. Important for butterflies and nearby birds.  
Coatings| Leica coatings improve contrast and color; AquaDura repels water and dirt from outer lenses.
