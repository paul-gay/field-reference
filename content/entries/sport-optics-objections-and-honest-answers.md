They say| Answer  
---|---  
"Why so much for binoculars?"| "Let's compare them side by side. Brightness at dusk, color and edge sharpness are where the difference shows, and good binoculars last a lifetime."  
"8x or 10x?"| "For most people, 8x is easier to hold steady and find things with. 10x is great if you look at distant, still subjects."  
"I don't need anything big."| Show the Noctivid Compact or Monovid.
