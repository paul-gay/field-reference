## The Story
**The original Leica view.** 50mm is where Leica began: Max Berek's first lens for Barnack's camera was a 50.

**Designed by Peter Karbe.** The longtime head of Leica optical design created the APO-Summicron-M 50, regarded as one of the most highly corrected 50mm lenses ever made.

**Why it matters now.** Its precision shows most on today's high resolution sensors.

## The Craft
### Outer strengths
- Remarkably small for its performance.
- Clean, modern barrel with a focus tab.
- Uses the 50mm frame lines.

### Inner strengths
- **Apochromatic correction**: colors focus at the same point, so edges stay clean.
- **Aspherical elements** for sharpness across the frame.
- Clinical precision even wide open at f/2.
