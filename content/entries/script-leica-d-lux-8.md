> **Catchphrase**
>
> "This is the Leica you can take everywhere: a bright Summilux zoom and a real sensor, small enough for a jacket pocket."

**Then pause.** Ask where it will go: travel, family, a gift. That decides everything else.

## The walkthrough
| Step | Say |
|---|---|
| Description | A compact with a Four Thirds sensor and a 24 to 75mm equivalent Summilux zoom. |
| Inspiration | Leica's compact line brings Leica design and color to everyday cameras. |
| Features | A much bigger sensor than a phone, so it's better in low light. Wide to portrait in one twist. The same clean menu as our Q. |
| Style | Classic, simple, pocketable. |
| Other | A thoughtful gift, and often someone's first Leica. |

## Adapt the opening
- **Traveler:** One small camera for the whole trip.
- **Phone photographer:** Let's compare it with your phone in the darkest corner.
- **Gifter:** Tell me about who it's for.

## Put in their hands
Zoom from wide to 75mm on the same scene, then compare with their phone.

## Skip unless asked
Sensor size debates; be honest that it's developed with a partner if asked.

## Scene: Tom, shopping for his mother's birthday
- **Tom:** She takes everything on her phone.
- **You:** Does she travel?
- **Tom:** Every spring.
- **You:** This is the Leica you can take everywhere...
