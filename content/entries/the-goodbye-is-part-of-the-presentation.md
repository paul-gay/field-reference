## Close the visit, not the sale  
  
  * **Summarize what they liked.** "It sounds like the Q3 43 felt right for portraits of your kids."
  * **Offer a reason to return that isn't buying.** A photo walk, an Akademie workshop, an exhibition, a gallery opening, or a test drive of a camera over a weekend if the program is available.
  * **Ask permission to stay in touch.** "Would you like me to let you know about the next event?" Capture details in the CRM only with consent.
  * **Give something to carry away.** Your card, a gallery program, or a Sofort print.
  * **Walk them to the door.** A small gesture that signals hospitality.

## The follow up

Within a few days, send a short personal note that references something specific from the visit. "Great to meet you on Saturday. Here's the workshop I mentioned." No pressure, no price list. Then log what they shoot, what they held, and what they'd want next, so whoever sees them next can continue the conversation.

> **The long view**
>
> The student who held an M today may be buying one in five years. Leica clients are often lifelong, and people remember the advisor who treated them well before they could afford anything.
