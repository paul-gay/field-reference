* Choose a 35mm or 50mm lens. Set it to f/5.6 or so, so the client doesn't struggle with extreme shallow focus.
  * Put the strap on their wrist or neck first.
  * "Look through this window. See the small bright patch in the middle? Point it at my face."
  * "Now turn this ring slowly until the two images become one." Wait. Let them feel it click into place.
  * "Notice you can see outside those bright lines? That's where the next photo is coming from."
  * Let them take a photo, then show it on the rear screen at full magnification. The detail from 60 megapixels usually gets a reaction.
  * For hesitant clients, show zone focusing or hand them the M EV1 to compare.

> **Pro move**
>
> Put an M11 and an M EV1 side by side with the same lens. Let the client choose. There's no wrong answer, and letting them decide builds confidence in the purchase.
