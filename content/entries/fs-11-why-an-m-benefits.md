## The system
- **One mount since 1954,** which means your lenses keep their value and work on every M, film or digital.
- **Small full frame body, small lenses,** which means a serious camera that doesn't intimidate. People stay natural.
- **Manual focus, simple controls,** which means you make every decision, and you learn photography faster.
- **Hand assembled, built to be serviced,** which means an instrument for decades, not a gadget for a few years.

| Model | Pitch | Feature → benefit |
|---|---|---|
| M EV1 | "The M for people who want to see exactly what they'll get." | Built in EVF → exact frame and focus with any lens. Peaking with the focus ring → nail focus on a Noctilux wide open. ~495g → all day carry. Content Credentials → authentic images. |
| M11-P | "Everything the M does, with nothing left out." | Optical rangefinder → see outside the frame; no lag. 256GB built in → thousands of DNGs and a backup. Content Credentials. Sapphire screen → stays clear for years. |
| M11 Glossy Black Paint | "The M that becomes yours as you use it." | Paint on brass → wears to gold where your hands go. Brass top and dials → classic weight. Same 60MP sensor. Crosshatch knurled dials. |
| M11-D | "A digital camera that shoots like film." | No rear screen → stay in the moment. ISO dial → settings readable even switched off. 256GB built in. Content Credentials. |
| M11 Monochrom | "Black and white without compromise." | Sensor sees only light → sharper files, richer tones. Clean high ISO. No color decisions. All black, no markings. |
| M6 | "The most loved film Leica, made new." | Built in meter with arrows. Brand new → warranty, fresh shutter. Brass with black paint → ages like an original. Same lenses as digital. |
| MP 0.72 | "The professional's film M." | Mechanical shutter, battery only for the meter. Black paint on brass patina. 0.72 finder → frame lines 28 to 135. Meter included. |
| M-A (Typ 127) | "A camera that will outlive us all." | No battery, no electronics → works anywhere, any decade. No meter → learn to read light. Chrome plated brass. A true heirloom. |

> **The bridge phrase**
>
> "Which means..." turns every spec into a reason. "60 megapixels, which means you can crop a 28mm shot to a 50mm frame and still print it big."
