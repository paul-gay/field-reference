Leica Mobile brings Leica's imaging know how to smartphones in two ways. First, through partnerships with phone makers, most recently Xiaomi, which produced the Leica Leitzphone powered by Xiaomi. Second, through Leica's own iPhone ecosystem: the Leica LUX camera app and physical accessories like the LUX Grip and LUX cases.

In fiscal 2025/26 Leica reported that its Mobile division continued to record strong growth. For the store, mobile is a bridge: it lets someone who isn't ready for a camera still own a piece of Leica today.
