## The 20 second version

> **Say it like this**
>
> "The M is the camera Leica is famous for. You focus it by hand through a rangefinder, which sounds hard but takes about a minute to learn. It's small, silent and completely in your control, and the same design has been used by great photographers since 1954."

## The two minute version

> **Say it like this**
>
> "Most cameras try to do everything for you. The M does the opposite. It gives you a bright window onto the world, a focusing patch, and beautiful lenses, and then gets out of the way. Because you're not looking through the lens, you see what's about to happen outside the frame. That's why photographers like Cartier-Bresson worked this way.
>
> Today's M11 has a 60 megapixel sensor, so the files are extraordinary, but the experience is the same one photographers have loved for 70 years. And the lenses you buy now will work on future M cameras and on our film cameras too. People don't just buy an M; they build a system over years."

## Words that help

  * "Intentional" and "in your control" rather than "manual" and "difficult."
  * "A system you build over years" rather than "expensive lenses."
  * "Made by hand in Wetzlar" as a fact, not a slogan.
