> **Your fifteen second intro**
>
> "Ernst Leitz trained as a watchmaker before his company built microscopes, and later cameras. Leica watches are made the same way as the cameras: precise, mechanical, and designed to be used. There are two families, Swiss automatic and German hand wound."

**Read the name, know the watch:** Leica uses no reference numbers. Collection + Case Material + Dial Name + Strap. Example: ZM 11 Steel Midnight Blue.

## Ask, show, answer
- **Ask:** Do you own a Leica camera? What's on your wrist today? Daily wear or special piece? Wind it yourself or automatic?
- **Show:** press the ZM 1 crown and watch the red dot appear for setting mode; swap a ZM 11 strap in seconds; hold the dial next to an M11.
- **"Leica makes watches?"** The founder trained as a watchmaker in 1858. It's an instrument company first.
- **"For $7,000 I'd get an Omega."** Fair, and Omega is excellent. This is for someone who wants something rarer, with a Swiss movement and Leica design few people wear.

## Accuracy traps
ZM 12 is LA-3002. The red dot on the ZM 1 and 2 shows the crown mode; it isn't a pusher. Leica's own sources differ on hand wound calibre names. Prices vary by source; quote "from."
