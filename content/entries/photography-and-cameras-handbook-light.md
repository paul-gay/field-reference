Quality| What it means  
---|---  
Hard light| Small, direct source (midday sun, bare flash). Crisp shadows, strong texture.  
Soft light| Large, diffused source (overcast sky, window, softbox). Gentle shadows, flattering skin.  
Direction| Front light flattens; side light reveals texture; back light creates rim light and silhouettes.  
Golden hour| The hour after sunrise and before sunset: warm, low, soft light.  
Blue hour| Just before sunrise and after sunset: cool, even light with city lights glowing.  
Inverse square law| Doubling the distance from a light cuts its intensity to a quarter.  
Color temperature (Kelvin)| Source  
---|---  
about 1,900K| Candlelight  
about 2,700 to 3,200K| Household tungsten bulbs  
about 5,500K| Daylight, flash  
about 6,500 to 7,500K| Overcast sky  
about 9,000K and up| Open shade, blue sky  
  
**White balance** tells the camera what "white" is under a light source so colors look natural. Raw files let you change it later without loss.

## Flash basics

  * **TTL** flash meters through the lens for automatic power.
  * **Guide number** rates flash power.
  * **High speed sync** lets focal plane cameras use flash above sync speed, with less power.
  * **Bounce** the flash off a ceiling or wall to soften it.
  * **Fill flash** lightens shadows in bright sun.
