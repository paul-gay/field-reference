> **20 seconds**
>
> "Leica's founder trained as a watchmaker before he ever made a lens, so watches bring the company full circle. These are mechanical watches designed like our cameras: precise, minimal, and a little bit ingenious."

> **The ZM 1 in two minutes**
>
> "Look at the power reserve. Those two blades close as the watch runs down, just like a lens aperture. Now press the crown. Instead of pulling it out, you push it, and the seconds hand snaps to zero so you can set the time precisely, and this little indicator shows you're in setting mode. It's hand wound, with about 60 hours of reserve, so winding it becomes a small daily ritual, the way a photographer loads film."

## Camera to watch bridges

Camera idea| Watch equivalent  
---|---  
Aperture blades| Power reserve blades on ZM 1 and ZM 2  
Manual focus M| Hand wound ZM 1 and ZM 2  
Autofocus Q| Automatic ZM 11 and ZM 12  
Red dot| Red dot strap fasteners on the automatic line  
Monochrom cameras| ZM Monochrom versions
