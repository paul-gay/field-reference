## The Story
**Why 43mm.** A 43mm lens sees the world close to how the eye does: natural, undramatic, flattering to people.

**September 2024.** The Q3 43 joined the Q family with Leica's first APO lens on a Q, in the same body as the Q3.

**The family.** One body, three expressions: Q3 (28mm), Q3 43, Q3 Monochrom.

## The Craft
### Outer strengths
- The same minimal Q3 body and dials.
- A discreet engraving on the hot shoe tells it apart.
- IP52 weather sealing, tilting touchscreen.

### Inner strengths
- **APO-Summicron 43 f/2 ASPH.**: 11 elements, 7 aspherical surfaces, image stabilized.
- **60MP** with Triple Resolution.
- **In camera framing** at 60, 75, 90, 120 and 150mm; macro focus to 27cm.

## In Hand
### See
- A portrait at 43mm next to the same one at 28mm.
- The framing lines stepping in to 90mm.

### Touch
- The macro ring on the APO lens.
- The same Q controls, nothing new to learn.

### Feel
- A natural view that never distorts faces.
- Reach for portraits without changing lenses.

## In one frame
The Q for people who photograph people: a natural 43mm view, APO precision, and reach up to 150mm in one camera.

## Choose it if
- You mostly photograph people.
- 28mm feels too wide for you.
- You want portrait reach in a compact.
