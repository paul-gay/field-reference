* Let the client wind the ZM 1 and watch the power reserve blades open.  
  * Demonstrate the push crown and seconds reset.
  * On the ZM 2, show the GMT hand for a second time zone, great for travelers.
  * On the ZM 11 or 12, change the strap in seconds.
  * Place a watch next to an M camera on a tray. The design kinship sells itself.
