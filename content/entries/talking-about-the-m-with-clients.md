If the client...| Use this  
---|---  
Loves history| The 1954 M3 story and the unbroken mount.  
Is a watch person| The M as a hand wound mechanical watch; the M-A as a watch with no battery at all.  
Is nervous about manual focus| The four week plan compressed: "Try zone focusing right now."  
Shoots film already| The M6, MP and M-A, all made new today.  
Wants the M but can't focus reliably| The M EV1, with focus peaking and magnification.  
Collects| Serial numbers, editions, Classic Line lenses, and the auction story.
