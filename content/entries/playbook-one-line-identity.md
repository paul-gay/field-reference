## The technique
Before any facts, each family has an identity line of a few words: its personality in one breath. It frames the whole conversation and is easy for the client to repeat to friends.

Good identity lines are:
- **Short**, a few words.
- **Emotional**, not technical.
- **True**, rooted in the product's real history.
- **Distinct** from every other family's line.
