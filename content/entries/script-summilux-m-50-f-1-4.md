> **Catchphrase**
>
> "Same focal length as the APO 50, completely different soul: the classic Leica glow, made on purpose."

**Then pause.** Show both 50s side by side and let them describe the difference.

## The walkthrough
| Step | Say |
|---|---|
| Description | A 50mm f/1.4 from the Classic Line, in brass. |
| Inspiration | It uses the traditional seven element spherical formula, the look of earlier decades. |
| Features | Soft and glowing wide open, much sharper at f/5.6 or f/8. |
| Style | Vintage heart, modern build. |
| Other | Beautiful on a Monochrom or with film. |

## Adapt the opening
- **Black and white shooter:** This is made for your Monochrom.
- **Film lover:** It feels like the photographs you grew up with.
- **Collector:** The Classic Line is Leica honoring its own history.

## Put in their hands
One portrait with each 50, same light, same spot.

## Skip unless asked
Why there's no ASPH., unless they ask; then it's the story.
