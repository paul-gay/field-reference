## The principles
- Values should come through **naturally** in conversation, never as a list.
- Use **examples**: the people who wear or use the product, partnerships, how pieces are made.
- Be **proud and honest, never arrogant.** Modest, open and welcoming, whoever the client is.
- Help clients feel part of the family.
- The store itself should communicate the values before anyone speaks.

## How it transfers to Leica
| Leica value | Show it with |
|---|---|
| The essential (*Das Wesentliche*) | The Monochrom: a camera that does less on purpose. |
| Heritage | The M bayonet, unchanged since 1954. |
| Craft | About 45 M cameras a day in Wetzlar. |
| Bearing witness | Cartier-Bresson, Korda, the gallery wall. |
| Character | The Leica Freedom Train, told with care. |
