> **Catchphrase**
>
> "If you could only have one M lens, this is the argument: a 35 that works from morning light to candlelight."

**Then pause.** Ask where their photos happen. After dark means this, not the Summicron.

## The walkthrough
| Step | Say |
|---|---|
| Description | A fast 35mm f/1.4 that focuses as close as 0.4 meters. |
| Inspiration | Leica's fast 35 has been a night favorite of documentary photographers for decades. |
| Features | Soft and atmospheric wide open, crisp from about f/4. You'll feel a stop at 0.7 meters where the rangefinder hands off to live view. |
| Style | Two personalities in one lens. |
| Other | Pairs perfectly with the M EV1 for close work. |

## Adapt the opening
- **Night shooter:** This is your lens.
- **Undecided:** Let me take the same photo at f/1.4 and f/5.6.
- **Summicron owner:** This is the step up for low light.

## Put in their hands
Shoot one frame at f/1.4 and one at f/5.6 and show both.

## Skip unless asked
Floating element explanations unless they ask how close focus works.

## Scene: Omar, who photographs his city at night
- **Omar:** My photos fall apart after sunset.
- **You:** If you could only have one M lens...
