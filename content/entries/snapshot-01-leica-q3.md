## The Story
**The idea since 1913.** Oskar Barnack built the first Leica so a serious camera could go everywhere with you. The Q takes that idea literally: one superb lens, permanently attached.

**2015 · The first Q.** A full frame camera with a fixed Summilux 28 and autofocus. It made Leica feel approachable without compromising the picture.

**2023 onward · The Q3 family.** The Q3 brought a 60MP sensor, a tilting screen and 8K video. One body in three expressions: Q3, Q3 43 and Q3 Monochrom.

## The Craft
### Outer strengths
- Minimal top plate with physical dials.
- The Summilux 28 with its focus tab and macro ring.
- Tilting touchscreen and IP52 weather sealing.

### Inner strengths
- **60MP full frame sensor** with Triple Resolution: 60, 36 or 18MP.
- **Summilux 28 f/1.7 ASPH.**, designed for this sensor.
- **Hybrid autofocus**, fast and quiet.
- **Leaf shutter** in the lens: nearly silent.
