Leica Smart Projection, a subsidiary of Leica Camera AG, makes premium smart laser projectors for the home. Each uses a Leica Summicron lens, triple RGB laser light, and built in streaming, wrapped in Leica's minimalist aluminum and glass design. In fiscal 2025/26 Leica reported positive development in this business.

> **The heritage line**
>
> Leica developed its first projector for 35mm slides in 1926, one year after the Leica I. Projection is one of the brand's oldest businesses.
