Benefit| What it means  
---|---  
Zero delay| Focus is already done, so nothing stands between the moment and the shutter. Faster than any autofocus in sudden, close situations.  
Attention on the world| You watch people and light instead of a focus point or a screen.  
Discretion| You can raise the camera briefly, or not at all, without fiddling. People react less.  
Reliability| No autofocus hunting in low contrast or busy scenes.  
It suits the M| Rangefinder lenses have depth of field scales engraved on the barrel, made for exactly this.  
  
It's the classic technique of street and documentary photographers, and it's just as useful for children, parties, markets, festivals and travel: anywhere things happen fast and close.
