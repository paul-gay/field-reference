> **Your fifteen second intro**
>
> "This is the M. The mount hasn't changed since 1954, so a lens made in the fifties still fits this body. You focus it yourself, you see beyond the frame, and the camera stays out of the way. It's built to do one thing, stills, and to do it for a very long time."

**Every digital M shares:** 60MP full frame BSI sensor with Triple Resolution (60, 36 or 18MP), Maestro IV, BP-SCL7 battery, USB-C, manual focus only, no video, Leica FOTOS app.

## Ask first
- Have you used a rangefinder before?
- What do you photograph most?
- Do you like checking the screen, or would you rather not?
- Do you own M lenses already?

## Show them
- Summicron 35 on an M11-P. Have them focus on a face across the room until the two images merge.
- Same lens on the EV1. Let them feel the difference.
- Shoot a frame and open the DNG in FOTOS on their phone.

## If they say
- **"No autofocus?"** For many owners that's the point: you decide. The EV1 makes precise focus easy. If autofocus is a must, the Q3 or SL3-P is the right camera.
- **"That's a lot for a camera with no video."** It's built for stills only, and built to last. M bodies hold value, and your lenses move to every future M.
- **"Should I wait for the M12?"** Nothing has been announced. The lenses you buy today carry into whatever comes next.

## Pair it with
- Summicron-M 35 or 50
