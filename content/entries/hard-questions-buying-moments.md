*Help people decide, never corner them.*

### "I need to think about it."
- **Often behind it:** Real hesitation, or a polite exit
- **Say something like:** "Of course. What would help you decide?" Then offer a test drive, a hold or a follow up, per store policy.
- **Watch out:** Respect it. The best follow up is useful, not pushy.

### "My partner is going to kill me."
- **Often behind it:** Joking, or real guilt
- **Say something like:** Laugh with them. "Would it help to bring them in? Some of our best converts are skeptical partners."

### "You're just trying to sell me something."
- **Often behind it:** Distrust
- **Say something like:** "Selling is part of my job. So is making sure you leave with the right camera, even if that's the less expensive one."
- **Watch out:** Then prove it.

### "Do you work on commission?"
- **Often behind it:** Distrust
- **Say something like:** Answer honestly about how you're paid, then: "My goal is that you come back for years, not just today."

### "Can you hold it for me?"
- **Often behind it:** Needs time
- **Say something like:** "Let me check what we can do."
- **Watch out:** Follow the store's hold policy exactly.

### "What if I don't like it?"
- **Often behind it:** Buyer's remorse fear
- **Say something like:** "Let me walk you through our return policy and the test drive option."
- **Watch out:** Confirm the current policy; never improvise.

### "There's a waitlist? That's ridiculous."
- **Often behind it:** Frustration
- **Say something like:** "I understand. Production is small, and I'd rather be honest than promise a date I can't keep. I'll tell you exactly where you stand."
- **Watch out:** Apply the waitlist the same way for everyone.
