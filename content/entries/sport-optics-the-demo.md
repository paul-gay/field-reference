---

* Take the client to a window or doorway with a far target and a shaded area.
  * Show them how to set the eye cups (down for glasses wearers) and the diopter.
  * Compare 8x and 10x on the same target so they feel the difference.
  * For compacts, show how they fit in a jacket pocket or evening bag.
  * For digiscoping clients, show how a phone or camera attaches to a spotting scope, if the store has a setup.
