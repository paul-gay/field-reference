They say| Answer  
---|---  
"I can't change the lens."| "That's the design. Crop modes cover 35 to 90mm framing, and the lens is optimized for this sensor. If you truly need interchangeable lenses, let's look at the M or SL."  
"My phone does this."| "Phones are amazing in good light. The difference shows in low light, in real background blur from a big sensor and fast lens, and in large prints. Let's try it in a dim corner."  
"28 or 43, I can't decide."| Do the side by side portrait test. Ask which photos they already love.  
"Should I wait for a Q4?"| "Leica hasn't announced one. The Q3 family is current, and a camera you use now beats one you wait for."
