## Digital M (M11 family)  
  
  * Set ISO to Auto with a sensible maximum, and the shutter speed dial to A. The camera now handles exposure while you choose aperture and focus.
  * Choose an aperture: f/2 for shallow depth and low light, f/5.6 to f/8 for more in focus.
  * Raise the camera to your eye. Put the focus patch on your subject.
  * Turn the focus ring until the double image merges into one.
  * Recompose if needed and press the shutter gently.
  * For very shallow depth of field or close subjects, use live view with focus peaking on the rear screen, or an EVF such as the Visoflex.

## Film M (M6 or MP)

  * Remove the baseplate and load the film: insert the cassette, pull the leader across to the take up spool, replace the baseplate.
  * Advance and fire two frames until the counter reads 1.
  * Set the film speed (ISO) on the camera to match the film.
  * Look through the viewfinder. The LED arrows show which way to turn the aperture or shutter; a balanced exposure lights both or a center dot, depending on the model.
  * Focus with the rangefinder, shoot, advance.
  * At the end of the roll, press the rewind release, rewind with the crank, and remove the baseplate to unload.
  * On an M-A there's no meter: use a handheld meter, a phone app, or the sunny 16 rule.
