## The Story
**The Classic Line.** Recreations of historic Leica designs, for photographers who want character, not only perfection.

**No ASPH. on purpose.** This Summilux uses the traditional seven element spherical formula, in brass, just as earlier generations did.

**A modern choice.** A deliberate alternative to the Summilux-M 50 ASPH.

## The Craft
### Outer strengths
- Brass construction with a classic feel.
- Traditional engravings and scales.
- The 50mm frame lines.

### Inner strengths
- **Seven element spherical design**, no aspherical elements.
- Soft, glowing rendering wide open.
- Much sharper stopped down to f/5.6 or f/8.

## In Hand
### See
- Highlights that bloom softly wide open.
- The same scene at f/8, suddenly crisp.

### Touch
- The weight and finish of brass.
- The classic focus ring.

### Feel
- A connection to the photographs of earlier decades.
- Character you can't add later in editing.
