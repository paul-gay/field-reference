## Ask first
Is this for you or a gift? Where will you take it? Do you mostly shoot on your phone today?

## Show them
- Print one of the customer's own phone photos on the Sofort 2.
- Install LUX on their iPhone and try a Leica Look. It's free.
- Zoom the D-Lux 8 from 24 to 75 and pop the flash on.

## If they say
- **"Isn't the D-Lux 8 a Panasonic?"** They share a platform, as Leica compacts have for years. The Leica has its own lens tuning, color, menu, design, DNG files, and Leica's service.
- **"Why pay more for an instant camera?"** You only print what you love, so film stops going to waste, and it doubles as a printer for your phone.

**Phones:** the Leica Leitzphone powered by Xiaomi launched in March 2026 but isn't sold in the US. **Retired models customers mention:** D-Lux 7, V-Lux 5, C-Lux, original Sofort.
