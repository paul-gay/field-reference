* Help the client download LUX on their own phone if they're interested, and apply a Leica Look to a photo they take in the store.
  * Put the LUX Grip on the demo iPhone and let them feel the shutter.
  * Photograph the same scene with LUX and then a Q3. Let the difference start a conversation, without disparaging the phone.

> **Why this matters**
>
> A visitor who leaves with LUX on their phone has Leica in their pocket every day. That's a relationship, not a lost sale.
