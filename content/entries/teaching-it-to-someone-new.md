> **Script**
>
> "I've set this camera so everything from about six feet to over twenty feet will be sharp. You don't need to focus at all. Just point it toward the door and take pictures as people walk by."  
>   
> Preset: 35mm lens, f/8, 3 m (10 ft), fast shutter, Auto ISO.

  * Let them shoot five or six frames touching only the shutter.
  * Show the frames at full magnification.
  * Explain the bread slice in one sentence.
  * Show them the two 8s on the lens barrel.
  * Close: "The great street photographers set the camera once, then paid attention to people instead of the camera."

> **If they ask "why not autofocus?"**
>
> "Autofocus is great, and the Q3 has excellent autofocus. Zone focusing wins when things happen suddenly and close to you. There's zero delay because focus is already done."
