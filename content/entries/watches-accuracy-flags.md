* The ZM 12 calibre is LA-3002, not LA-3001 (a common press error). The ZM 11 is LA-3001.  
  * Leica's own sources are inconsistent on hand wound calibre naming; say "the ZM 1 and ZM 2 calibre" rather than a code.
  * Don't conflate the 1986 renaming of the Leitz company with any watch launch.
  * Only the ZM 2 has GMT and day/night.
  * Confirm current prices and variants in store systems.
