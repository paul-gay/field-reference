Topic| Explanation  
---|---  
PPI and print size| At 300 pixels per inch, a 60MP file (about 9,500 x 6,300 pixels) prints roughly 32 x 21 inches with no upscaling. Large prints viewed from farther away need fewer PPI.  
Paper| Glossy (punchy), luster or semi gloss (versatile), matte and fine art cotton rag (subtle, archival), baryta (a traditional darkroom feel).  
Inkjet and giclée| Pigment inkjet prints on fine art paper, often sold as giclée.  
Darkroom prints| Silver gelatin prints made from negatives with an enlarger.  
Soft proofing| Previewing on screen how an image will look on a given paper and printer.  
Framing| Mats give breathing room; UV protective glazing preserves prints.  
Screens| Phones and monitors show a different brightness and color range from paper; prints usually need slightly brighter editing.
