**Zone focusing** means setting the lens in advance so a whole band of distance is sharp, then shooting anything that happens inside that band without focusing each frame.

> **The bread slice**
>
> "What's in focus is like a slice of bread cut through the scene in front of you. Inside the slice is sharp; in front or behind is soft. Normally you move that slice with the focus ring for every photo. Zone focusing means you decide where the slice sits and how thick it is ahead of time, then stop thinking about focus and just watch for the moment."

The focus ring decides **where** the slice sits. The aperture decides **how thick** it is. That's the whole idea; everything else in this guide is detail and practice.
